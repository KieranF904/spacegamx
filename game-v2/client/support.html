Hey! Thank you for playing my game! I spend countless hours on this project, and pay to maintain the servers, website, etc.

Currently we have no monetization: no ads, no pay to win, nothing. All donations to the project are tracked and associated with your account.

We may remove the rank for abuse, or at our discretion, and may give it for free to players as we see fit.
