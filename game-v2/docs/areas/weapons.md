# Weapons & combat
How a gun turns into shells, beams and pulses, and what those do to ships. The firing step (`stepGuns`) and the shell launch (`launchShot`) are deterministic and shared, so a pilot's client builds the same volley the server does from a bare fire event. Damage, shields, splash, EMP, burns, point defence and the Repair Beam live in the server's `ShipBridge` and `Weapons`. Hit tests against part outlines are shared with client beam visuals. Weapon *parts* (models, grades, mounts) belong to `ship-parts`; this area is what they do once they fire.

## Public interface
- `WeaponTuning` / `ShipTuning` (tuning.ts). Kinds in tuning.json `weapons`: cannon railgun flak missile emp laser autocannon pulse arc torpedo mortar magma charge lance hive frostbite repair point_defence, plus boss kinds rivet scrap_chunk seam_seeker heart_spark heart_seeker cinder_bloom prism prism_shard phase singularity.
- `stepGuns(ship, pose, orders: GunOrders, tick, tuning)`, `launchShot(...)`, `gunDef` / `partWeaponDef`, `weaponDef(tuning, kind)`, `rebuildVolley(FireRecord, ...)` (client rebuild from a fire event).
- `weaponHit(kind, def, grade): HitInfo`, `hitMultiplier(catalog, tuning, part, hit)`.
- `ShipBridge.hurt(eid, ship, part, amount, source, vs?)`, `splashAt`, `empAt(...)` (returns hit eids), `ignite`, `emp`, `frostHit`, `shieldFraction`, `lastHitter`.
- `Weapons.spawn(owner, kind, x, y, angle, damageScale, shape, out?)` used by ShipBridge (pilots, mobs) and brains (bosses). `WeaponsHost` is what GameServer supplies.
- `castParts(geoms, ok, x0, y0, x1, y1, r?)` for any new hit test.

## Invariants
- Shell spawn is deterministic from `(owner, partUid, index, tick, aim quantised to SHOT_AIM_STEPS)`; never use `Math.random`/`Date.now` in guns.ts, launch.ts, shot.ts. Server and client must derive the same `gunDef` (grade, size, mods, ammo, model, braced).
- All damage goes through `ShipBridge.hurt` (shields, stage cover, bane, invulnerable). Do not decrement part HP directly.
- Hit tests use real part outlines in grid space (`ShipBridge.worldToGrid`), box first, convex pieces second; not bounding circles (collisions.md). Mobs are circles by design.
- A kind with no `WEAPON_KIND_TYPE` entry goes out as type 1 (cannon).
- Beams (laser, lance, frostbite, repair) are continuous, live in ShipBridge and send no shells.
- Point defence never fires on the trigger and never at ships; only kinds with `intercept > 0` can be shot down (boss novas opt out via `shape.intercept`).
- Dry ammo: same shells, no effects (splash/emp/ignite/split/pull/pierce/fuse stripped) at the `dry` share of damage, so tracers stay in sync.
- Stage rule: a hit on a later-stage part passes to the nearest current-stage part measured from the impact point, at `stageCover` (default 0.4).

## How to extend
1. **Tweak a kind's numbers**: data/ships/tuning.json `weapons.<kind>`. A new field goes in `WeaponTuning` (tuning.ts) and is read in `Weapons.ts`/`launch.ts`; gems/ammo can only touch `WeaponModNumber` fields.
2. **Add a projectile kind**: (a) tuning.json `weapons.<kind>`; (b) `TYPE_BY_KIND` in damage.ts if not kinetic; (c) `WEAPON_KIND_TYPE` + `WeaponType` enum in common/src/ecs/components.ts (new number); (d) `WEAPON_STYLES` in weaponStyles.ts; (e) a weapon model in scripts/econ/models.mjs (read only its weapon rows, ~L34-80; see `ship-parts`), then `npm run wire:sync` and `wire:bump "reason"`; (f) barrel art in gunDecor.ts (`BY_DECOR`, a `<kind>_<model>` entry); (g) sfx in client/src/audio/sfx.ts. Only special behaviour needs Weapons.ts (`tick` for homing/pull/fuse, `onShipHit`, `detonate`).
3. **Add a beam**: copy `fireBeam` in bridge/weapons.ts (hit via `castParts` capsule, damage via `hurt`); draw it client-side like MiningBeamFx/WeaponFx. No `WEAPON_KIND_TYPE` needed.
4. **Add a damage type**: `DamageType`, `DAMAGE_TYPES`, `DAMAGE_NAMES` in damage.ts, material `resist` in materials.json, `TYPE_BY_KIND`.
5. **Add an on-hit effect**: field in `WeaponTuning`, applied in `Weapons.onShipHit` (`chain`, `shatter`, `detonate` are templates); cold effects go via `ShipBridge.warm`/`frostHit`.
6. **Support weapon**: pure logic in support.ts, loop in bridge/weapons.ts (`repairBeam`, `pointDefence`).

## Weapon groups (docs/design/groups-mines-docking.md section 1)
Every weapon is in any of four groups (`PlacedPart.wg`, a 4-bit mask, bit 0 = group 1; no `wg` = group 1 only). The pilot has two selections, the group the left button fires and the one the right button fires, each `all` (0) or 1-4, both starting on `all` (= fires as before groups). With the right selection on a group, the right button fires that group and a mining ship does not mine until it is set back to `all`. Pure helpers in `common/src/ships/weaponGroups.ts` (`partWeaponGroups`, `packGroupSel`, `gunTriggeredBy`, `trigWord`, `gunSelected`).
- **State**: the selection is one input byte (`ClientInputMessage.weaponGroups`, ECS `Input.weaponGroups`), sent only on the input it changes on and on the first input after a welcome or respawn (`client/src/app/inputSend.ts`, `ships/weaponGroups/selection.ts` `due/sent/forget`); the server keeps it until an input changes it (handlers/flight.ts). `ShipState.trig` / `GunState.trig` is the trigger word the guns stepped on last tick (left 1, right 2, selection << 2); `triggerHeld` stays the ship-wide "some gun button was down" (the held aim distance, the aim reads).
- **Step**: `stepGuns` takes `orders.word` (absent: left button when `firing`, selection all: an AI's). Each gun: `want = gunTriggeredBy(mask, word, hasMiners) && canFire`; a turret slews only while that gun's own trigger is held through from last tick's word (`pointTurretAim` otherwise); a semi-automatic gun answers a click (some gun's trigger going down) only while its groups are selected (`gunSelected`). `gunControls(ship, fire, fireAlt, stopped, sel)` returns `{firing, word, mining}`; mining is `fireAlt && hasMiners && !rightFiresGuns`. A pair of same-kind semi-automatic guns still takes turns (one shot a click); a selection change while held that adds an overlapping semi gun to the selection clicks the group's guns that are ready (not tracked per gun).
- **Other ends**: the server's `stepWeapons` builds the word from `Input.fireLeft/fireRight/weaponGroups`; own prediction carries `OwnInputExtras.sel` (`OwnTick.noteInput` keeps the last selection for the inputs that carry none); other ships' lanes read it from the gun row's bits (`GunNet.pilotCtl`: bits are the trigger word, a held trigger is `bits & TRIG_BUTTONS`, so a selection alone does not make the aim distance exact), and the lane hash and `gunSync` carry `trig`. The ring hash adds `trig` only once a group is selected.
- **Editing**: hangar (hover a weapon, 1-4 toggles that group, saved with the design: `client/src/ships/weaponGroups/editor.ts`) and the field build overlay (same keys, a `weaponGroups` message: `server/src/net/handlers/weaponGroups.ts` refits the live ship with the new groups, updates the saved record, and the design hash changes so every client resyncs the ship; refused over unsaved plan edits on the client). A weapon is never in no group.
- **HUD**: `L: 2  R: all` chip under the weapon ring (shown once a group is selected or a weapon is in another group), groups outside both selections dim, a state dot (ready / reloading / short of power) on the left group's segments when a group is selected (`ships/gunHud.ts`, `ui/WeaponRing.ts`).
- Tests: `common/src/ships/weaponGroups.guns.test.ts`, `common/src/sim/weaponGroups.parity.test.ts` (server step, own prediction and another client's lane agree across a selection change mid-burst), `server/src/net/handlers/weaponGroups.test.ts`, `client/src/app/inputSend.test.ts` (selection sent once per change).

## Rates and round speeds (docs/design/heavier-shots-2026-11.md)
Fewer, heavier rounds, balanced on landed damage (through armour and part HP, at moving targets), not paper DPS; round speed set by role (fast for precision guns; slow rounds hit for 88+ or burst). Applied 2026-11-07 in scripts/econ/models.mjs (W rows' `weaponMods.set`, the `FIRE` blocks); no kind in tuning.json changed, so enemy designs and boss brains fire as before. Per model now: Pip 18 dmg every 20 t at 54; Hammer 88 every 60 t at 38; Twinlink a clip of 8 at 10 t, 20 dmg at 62, reload 80; Rattler 11 every 15 t at 62; Chainsaw 11 at 54, spinning 24 -> 8 t; Stinger a clip of 6 at 12 t, 12 dmg at 88, reload 60; Strobe a 2-bolt burst of 14 at 84 every 30 t; Overpulse a clip of 6 at 10 t, 13 dmg at 96, reload 60; Coilgun 3 slugs of 25 in +-3 deg every 54 t; Rail Driver a semi clip of 4 at 12 t, 60 dmg; Leviathan 10 u/tick (and maxSpeed 10: a shell that neither homes nor steers never accelerates). Lives keep each round's reach. Energy a second stays: a cycle gun's `demand` and `intake` are unchanged (the Chainsaw's demand 10.5 -> 22.4 for its slower top beat), clip guns keep `reloadE` per reload, augments cost more a shot (buffer = clip). Wards' Pip, Rattlers and Chainsaws (bulwark.json, hauler.json) land about as before with half the shells. Bench: `server/node_modules/.bin/tsx scripts/bench/heavierShots.ts --md` (stated vs landed DPS, hit rate, aim-distance updates; `--enemy`, `--pd`; `PROPOSAL` + `--after` for a trial in memory), about 2 s, no world.

## Dev weapons range (dev server only)
For balance work: `dev_range` is a private, empty system (data/dev/range.json, merged by server/src/data/devData.ts like the Rockery; production never reads it). Press **F9** (button "Go to Weapons Range") or **F10** (the Range panel) in a Vite dev build. Ten real enemy ships stand at 400/800/1400 u (start mobs, mid ships, a shielded Frost Warden, an elite, a Tallyman-class boss hull): `Enemies.spawnTarget` pins them and gives them no AI, so they never move, turn or shoot; they pay no XP, credits, loot, quests or achievements (`Enemies.onDestroyed` returns early for `dummy`), and `WeaponsRange` brings each back whole at its spot `respawnSeconds` after it dies. "Moving targets" sweeps them side to side (autopilot). Click any weapon model in the F10 list: `buildRangeRig` (common) makes a borrowed ship (`Pilots.devSwapShip`, never saved) carrying two of it, or two different ones with "A + B", sized from the model's piece and mounting rules (tested for every model in rangeRig.test.ts). "Use ideal ammo" (`Ammo.setIdeal`) makes every crate gun fire unlimited rounds of `idealCrate(kind)`: the crate with the highest direct damage multiplier (`1 + mods.mul.damage`), ties to the standard crate; nothing is opened or spent. The table (server messages `rangeHead` when the switches, guns or labels change, and a compact `rangeRow` per dummy whose numbers moved, checked 4 times a second, so an idle range sends nothing; client devRangeFeed.ts puts them together) gives per dummy hull, shield, damage in the last 5 s, dps and time-to-kill from the first hit. All of it is gated on `devTeleport` + `NODE_ENV !== 'production'` (GameServer builds `range` only then; `devRange` is refused otherwise). Tests: `npx vitest run server/src/game/weaponsRange.test.ts common/src/ships/rangeRig.test.ts`.

## Gotchas
- Weapon model ids are in the append-only wire dictionary (`common/src/network/wire-dictionary.json`); a new gun fails wireDictionary.test until `npm run wire:sync` then `wire:bump "reason"`.
- Splash, EMP, flak fuses and arc chains once used centroids or circles; use hull distance (`nearestTarget`, `nearestParts`) or bosses are immune near their edges.
- A plain cannon shell has no `extras` entry; any grade/gem change creates one (`def !== this.def(kind)`), so new `Extra` state must default sanely.
- Shield ripple must use the hit point, not the bounding rim.
- No gun has heat, and guns never read warmth (rework 2026-11): a beam burns as long as the power lasts and sputters when short (`BROWNOUT`). Frost and warmth effects go through warmth.ts.
- Gun state (ShipState `rounds`, `reload`, `charge`, `spin`) steps only in `stepGuns`, forward only, on the server and the pilot's client; the pilot row's gun-state slot corrects the client's after 30 ticks of disagreeing. A gun's energy goes through `gunLoad` (energy.ts `gunDraw`, by model): a cycle gun its shot's over its reload, a clip gun `reloadE` × missing / clip over its reload, a charge gun `chargeE` over its charge, an ammunition gun nothing. New firing behaviour belongs in a model's options, not a new model; the prediction-parity firing scenarios (server/src/golden/parity.test.ts) and the volley agreement test (server/src/net/replication.test.ts) must pass.
- The tree has uncommitted edits (models.test.ts, mounting.test.ts and others); never revert.

## Notes (moved from DEVELOPMENT.md and the old engine-map)
- **Repair Beam** (model `utility_repair_beam`, kind `repair` in tuning.json): a held turret beam (`heal` HP/s) that passes through ships it may not mend and mends the damaged parts nearest where it lands on the first friendly hull (`GameServer.canHeal`; `features/support repairBeam`, `repairAt`); never its own ship, a destroyed part or a shield. What it mends is noted in `server/src/ships/features/support/Support.ts`, the support-credit hook: a mended pilot's kills pay its supporters as its party, and a boss counts them among its winners.
- **Point Defence** (model `point_defence_turret`, `pointDefence {range, slew}`): `stepGuns` only reloads it; `features/support pointDefence` turns it to the nearest shot it can fire on (`Weapons.threats`: kinds with `intercept` hits, owners that may hurt it; a boss nova's never unless its phase says `intercept: true`) and each shot takes one hit (`Weapons.intercept`). Clients get an `intercept` message (a plain one, not in the wire tables) as a tracer and pop (`DamageFx.onIntercept`), and the turret turns to it (`ShipClient.stepPointDefenceVisuals`).
- **Fixed guns aim**: non-turret guns have a +-2 degree gimbal (`tuning.fixedGun.gimbalDeg`; fixed beams `beamGimbalDeg` 9; a weapon kind's own `gimbalDeg` over both: the Lance's 13.5), each aiming at the cursor point from its own muzzle (`stepFixedGunAim` in `turret.ts`; the offset is stored in `ship.aim`, `buildShot` turns the shot by it). It fires only while the target is within the gimbal plus `fireToleranceDeg` (0.5), or within that range of the angle from the ship's centre; a close target off a wing fires at the closest angle. Their barrels are drawn turned by it (ShipRenderer, about where they leave the hull; the muzzle flash and a beam start at the turned tip): your own as predicted, another pilot's stepped from the cursor it sends (ShipClient.stepTurretVisuals), a pilotless ship's from the aim its last shot left at (fire events' aimQ). Legacy fixed cannons (enemies', bosses') also get `fixedGun` shell scaling: `intervalScale` 1.5 (applied in `rebuild`), `speedScale` 0.7, `sizeScale` 1.5 (on `ProjectileSpec`; lifetime divided by the speed scale so range is unchanged); a model weapon's mount and numbers are its own (`partMount`). Projectile snapshot rows carry `data[7]` (shell size), `data[8]` (1 = turret) and `data[9]` (a shell that ends at a chosen point: its end tick, 0 none).
- **Turrets** (`mount: 'turret'`): base window +-`turret.halfArcDeg` around the facing; rays from the turret's centroid that cross another live part of the same ship are closed, and `rebuild` recomputes windows so they widen as cover is shot away. A turret fires only when aligned with the cursor and pointing through an open sector; all turrets aim parallel along the ship-to-cursor angle. `computeTurretWindow` checks each part's bounding circle before clipping.
  - *Slew only while firing* (2026-11-06, Kieran): a turret turns at `turret.slewRate` (0.09 rad a tick) anywhere in its base window only on a tick the trigger was held through from the tick before (`stepGuns`: `slewing = orders.firing && ship.triggerHeld`). Not firing, and on the tick the trigger goes down, it points straight at the aim (`pointTurretAim`: clamped to its base window, a full-circle turret anywhere) and carries nothing from the tick before. So what fires on a trigger-down depends only on that tick's aim, which lets the netcode send other pilots' idle aim distance only roughly (docs/areas/networking.md, other ships' guns). Gameplay: slew while firing is unchanged; an idle turret is always on the aim, so the first shot of a burst no longer waits for a turret to catch up (at 5° a tick it seldom did). Shared by the server's real ships, the own client's prediction, other ships' lanes and AI ships (`aiFires`' trigger, the same stepGuns on both ends: an engaged AI's turrets track its lead exactly while it holds fire, and fire on it at once). Point defence is untouched: it aims itself at server-only threats with its own slew (`features/support stepPointDefenceAim`), not hashed in lanes. EMP'd or mid-jump (`firing` false), a turret points at the aim too.
  - *Held aim distance* (2026-11-06, Kieran): with the trigger held through, a pilot's guns (`GunOrders.hold`: the server's `stepWeapons`, `stepShipGuns` for mode Player) aim on a distance held from earlier ticks (`ShipState.heldDist`) that takes the cursor's only on a gun's due tick (`distDue`): the tick its cooldown reaches 0 (it fires on the cursor exactly), the tick it crosses half its interval, every half interval while it waits loaded; a charge gun's fill and half-fill, a clip's reload end and half; a beam every tick; a semi-automatic gun on a waiting click. Idle and at trigger-down: the cursor's. AI ships aim on their own distance every tick. The shells that end at the aim fly the held distance (`GunStep.dist`); a guided shell steers to the cursor. Lets the netcode send a held cursor about twice a fire interval (networking.md, other ships' guns).
  - *Drawn turrets* ease after the simulated angle (client `ships/turretEase.ts`, ShipRenderer.drawTurrets; visual only): a time constant of `EASE_FRAMES` (3 frames, ~50 ms), so an aim that jumps (an idle turret on the cursor, another pilot's exact distance arriving at trigger-down after rough idle updates) never snaps the barrel. On a shot (`ShipRenderer.kick`) the drawn barrel is pulled within `FIRE_GAP` (3°) of the angle it fired at, so a shell never visibly leaves a barrel pointing elsewhere; the shell and muzzle flash come from the exact barrel. Another pilot's turrets are aimed (ShipClient.stepTurretVisuals) by the cursor distance its gun lane eases between rough updates (`OtherGuns.distAt`), else the status row's hundreds.
- **Shot scaling**: `buildShot` scales the base shot's damage by satisfaction (min 0.1); fire interval is scaled separately by `rebuild`. With both selections on `all` (the default) either mouse button fires every weapon (the right one the mining lasers, on a ship that has them); see Weapon groups below.
- **Part deaths, decay, repair** (ShipBridge): `partDeaths` (each tick before the rebuild) sends `partDestroyed` (`how`: blast, break, crumble) for every part that hit 0 HP; functions in `tuning.partBlast` blow up (core at once, others after a seeded delay), damaging intact parts on any body in radius with falloff, so generators can chain; whoever started a chain gets the kill credit. `decay`: a core-less body gives each part a crumble tick spread around `debris.decayTicks` (crumbling parts never blow up). `repairStep` (every `repair.intervalTicks`): a ship with a core within `repair.range` (= `BANK.stationRange`) of a station's edge is mended from its repair bank's deposits (and the free part's spare): each damaged part is paid once (`host.payBankRepair`, `repairBill`) when it starts to heal, then heals `BANK.stationHealPerSecond` (10%) of its max HP a second until whole (`BridgeCtx.healPaid`); one missing part is regrown every `BANK.stationPartSeconds` (2 s, `host.payBankRebuild`, the part's recipe inputs), nearest the core that touches the attached structure; paused for `combatPauseTicks` after damage; a part a field machine has claimed (`host.isClaimed`) is left to it. Nothing is free: a host with no pay hooks mends nothing, and a pilotless ship (no pilot behind the hook) is mended as before. `contact` messages (point, normal, closing speed) go above `collision.fxMinSpeed`, once per pair per `fxCooldownTicks`.
- **Legendary weapons**: functions `hive`, `frostbite`, `cinder_bloom`, `prism`, `phase`, `singularity` (tuning `weapons`: `burst`, `slow`, `ignite`, `split`, `pierceKeep`, `pull`); they open by level and each recipe needs its boss's artefact (a very rare drop, docs/parts-redesign.md section 6).
- **Combat rules**: splash only hits the few parts nearest the blast; EMPs leave 4 s of immunity; PvP only where `systems.json` says (`GameServer.canDamage`); wards (bastions, turrets, pylons, freighters) and docked ships cannot hurt or be hurt. Frost hits (gemmed guns, Brood Mother) also take the target's warmth (`ShipBridge.warm`, see world-sim). The Frostbite Lance no longer chills: it slows (below).
- **Beam ramp** (Cutter; tuning `ramp {to, ticks}`, set in `models.mjs` with the base `dps`): `fireBeam` multiplies the burn by `rampScale` as the beam stays on one part (a run kept per ship and gun in the bridge's `ramps`: the target part and ticks burned; a new part, or more than `RAMP_GRACE` ticks without a burn, starts it over; a pulse, a rock or empty space leaves it at the start). Ratings and part stats use `ramp.to`. Server-only: no wire.
- **Shot behaviours** (features/weaponry, rework 2026-11; models switch them on in models.mjs `SHOT`): `endAtAim` (Cloudmaker, Prism, Blackout) cuts a shell's life to the aim distance (launch.ts `aimReach`/`lifeToReach`, the mortar's maths) and it bursts or splits there (`onExpire`), or earlier on its fuse or impact; `walk` (Quake) fires shell i at `at[i]` × the aim distance, `gap` ticks apart (the bridge's `salvos` queue); `guided` (Hammerhead) steers toward the owner's aim point while its trigger is held (`ShipBridge.guideOf`, `Weapons.guide`); `mark` (Searchlight) makes `hurt` take `MARK_TAKEN` (×1.2) for `mark.ticks`; `slow` (Frostbite) flies the target with thrust and turning × `SLOW_CONTROL` (0.6) for `slow.ticks` (flight.ts `withSlowed`, applied after numbness and before EMP on the server, in the pilot's prediction from the pilot row's slowed ticks, and in replicas from `CTL.slowed`). Shells that end at the aim or are guided are sent as rows, not fire events (`firesStraight`); their row's `data[9]` is the tick they end (0 none). Statuses: pilot row extras `marked`, `slowed` (ticks left), status row `[7]` bits (marked 1, slowed 2), all pinned in features.wire.json.
- **Ordnance** (mortars, missiles, torpedoes; rework 2026-11 section 4; numbers in models.mjs W rows, FIRE intervals and SHOT): all ammo guns. Shot options on `WeaponTuning` beside `walk`: `salvo {count, gap}` (the Swarm's six rockets, 6 ticks apart; the rest queue in the bridge `salvos` list with the first's lock, and are sent as rows like any homing missile) and `arm` (ticks unarmed after launch: `Weapons.unarmed` makes the collision pass skip the shell; the Leviathan's 60). Point-defence hits are `intercept`, set in a model's mods (Hammerhead 2, Eel 3, Leviathan 5; kinds give 1). A homing missile locks on the ship nearest the cursor, else the selected target. `rating.ts` counts `walk` and `salvo` shells in a pull. Tests: common `ordnance.test.ts`, server `shotBehaviours.test.ts`.
- **Client weapon visuals** (`rendering/weapons/`): shots are placed from their exact spawn point and tick (snapshot `data[3..5]`, owner `data[6]`); your own run on the present timeline, others' on the interpolated one. **Where a shell stops**: every drawn shell (predicted, from a row or a fire event) stops at the first surface it touches as drawn, in that frame (`WeaponFx.castHit` -> `ships/drawnHit.ts` DrawnWorld: each frame's stretch of its flight, a bounding-circle test, then the standing parts' real outlines (`castParts`) at the pose the ship is drawn at, or a raised shield's bubble (DamageFx's circle), and rocks' outlines; never the shooter nor a ship its shells pass through on the server, `sidesHurt`; lobbed shells and a shell not yet armed fly on). Its impact goes off there (`onStruck`: shield ripples and plating sparks there too), its head stops and its tracer collapses into the point; nothing of it is drawn past it (an ended shell is kept, undrawn, `KEEP_ENDED_FRAMES` after its fade, then remembered by id, so a late row, hit or adopt never brings it back). Piercing shells mark each hull they pass and fly on. The server's hits only confirm: a hit on a shell that ended (or passed a hull the server now tells) changes nothing; one for a shell that hasn't touched anything drawn yet waits for its head as before, and if its head is there first it ends at the first drawn surface between its spawn and the server's point. Beams stop at hulls (MiningBeamFx castShips) and raised shields. Tests: visualStop.test.ts, drawnHit.test.ts; live proof docs/panels (projectile-surface-impacts). `projectileHit` (id + exact point) ends a tracer not drawn to a surface first; a hit ahead of the drawn head (another pilot's shell, drawn a few ticks behind the server) waits for the head to get there, and a hit that arrives before its shell's snapshot is matched to it on the next update. A hit on one of your own shells that no snapshot ever brought (fired and spent between two snapshots: most Spark bolts inside about 350, streams at point-blank; or made and gone in one coalesced message) takes the predicted shell on its line (`WeaponFx.adopt`: within 30 units, its head already past the point on your timeline, the oldest first); before that, no row claimed the prediction and it flew on through the target to the end of its reach (adoptHit.test.ts). A straight shell flies to the end of its flight on its timeline (`Shot.endAt`: spawn + life for fire-event and predicted shells, else about where the server dropped it), even after its removal comes, so a short-lived one (the Spark, 6 ticks) is still seen flying by other pilots; a shell fired and removed between two snapshots is removed with the next one (GameState `lateRemovals`). Impacts throw off the surface struck (`hitThrow`: `reflect` slugs and rails, `normal` pulse, arc, prism and phase bolts, default `back`) and lead with that throw: `ImpactLayers.flare` (bright streaks along the rebound line), a dense `spray`, loose sparks kept to `scatter` degrees of it; the centred core and glow are small and brief and directional hits have no shock ring (explosives keep their centred burst). Big hits also throw `fireball` (glows thrown out off the hull, cooling) and `debris` (fragments that burst out fast, slow, cool and trail smoke: speed and life drawn apart from a skewed spread round the typical, a share of outliers (`outliers`, `outSpeed`, `outLife`; embers too) much faster or longer-lived, carrying a heavy hit to 1.5-2 s); size and length rise with damage a shot and tier (`scripts/balance/impact-audit.ts` prints the table, `impactSpan.ts` measures, impactSpan.test.ts keeps the order). Held beams throw the same kind of impact every few frames while they burn (`BeamLook.burn`, MiningBeamFx `onBurn` to `WeaponFx.beamBurn`: Glint, Cutter, Tesla, Searchlight; the three lasers' lead with a liquid splash, `ImpactLayers.liquid` drawn by `rendering/weapons/liquidSplash.ts`: syrupy blobs arcing back to the surface and sticking as smears, a film creeping the way the beam slides and cooling to a crust, all riding the target): `ships/hitSurface.ts` casts the shell's line against the drawn parts and rocks near the hit (no network), `rendering/weapons/impactAim.ts` is the maths; pulse lasers' hits (PulseFx) splash out of the surface the same way. Each impact rides what it hit (`impactAnchor.ts`, one anchor per impact): hitSurface also says whose surface it was (`SurfaceNormal.target`; a single-body enemy or station by the nearest body round the point), and once a frame each anchor follows that entity's drawn pose (one lookup) and moves the impact's surface-bound parts (core, glow, fireball, shock rings, scorch, light, streaks, rings) with it; its free parts (sparks, sprays, embers, fragments, smoke, a fragment's smoke puffs) fly in the target's frame (`rideStep`): their velocity is relative to the target and drags to rest on it, and each frame they are carried by how far the hit point moved, so nothing smears round an orbiting rock or behind a ship that turns or brakes. `AnchorList` (open, hold, step once a frame) is shared: pulse-laser hits (PulseFx: sparks, droplets, rings, scorch), mining chips and dust (MiningFx, the rock the cast met) and plating hits (DamageFx) ride the same way. A target that's gone leaves its anchor coasting on its last smoothed velocity and spin (measured per move, so a rock placed once a tick reads steady at any frame rate). Look: thin additive tracers, brief muzzle flashes, spark bursts, pooled `SpritePool` sprites; guns recoil and heat (`onMuzzle` -> `ShipRenderer.kick`). A muzzle flash is `muzzle.size × muzzleScale(shell size × shotScale)` (in step through the usual range, floor 0.8, growing at 0.4 of the rate past 3). Warhead kinds (missile, torpedo, mortar, flak, EMP, hive, cinder bloom, singularity, charge) leave only a small `BURST_MARK` where the shell strikes; their explosion is DamageFx `onDetonation`. Audit and costs: docs/audits/weapon-fx-audit-2026-11-04.md. `BULLET_SPEED` is 48, `BLASTER_LIFE_TICKS` 150.
