# World, systems & sim LOD
The map and everything that keeps it alive cheaply: system/station/planet/asteroid data, travel (jump gates, rifts, pockets, surfaces), the two-tier simulation (live vs distant pilotless ships), world memory (systems nobody is in are written down and taken out), traffic and derelicts, per-system hazards and cold (warmth), and the client's map dots for far ships. Pilotless ships are cheap because most of them are "distant" (a closed-form route, no physics) until a pilot is near.

## Public interface
- `SimLod`: `LodHost` callbacks; `ShipBridge.wake`, `sleep(eid, recent)`, `sleepBlocker(eid, recent)`, `teleport`; `Enemies.intent`, `Bosses.busy` feed it.
- `SystemMemory` capture/restore through `Enemies.capture/restore`, `Bosses.capture/restore`, traffic and derelict memories; `worldFileStore` is the persistence seam (file today).
- Travel: `travelTo(conn, systemNum, x, y, keepMomentum)` (gates stop the ship, rifts keep momentum); `Surfaces.descend/toOrbit`; pocket helpers in common.
- Data lookups: `gameData.getSystemByNum`; `hazardsAt(rules, system, x, y)`.
- Wire: far-ship dots in snapshots (mapDots) and `FarShips` on the client.

## Invariants
- A distant ship's position is a pure function of its leg and the time; asking twice gives the same answer. Nothing hits it, it hits nothing; its parts, shield, battery and cooldowns are kept and caught up on `wake`.
- Never demote a busy ship (fighting, hurt or shot lately, EMP, burning, repairing, mid-jump, ward in an occupied system, boss mid-move).
- `PROMOTE_RADIUS` must stay beyond the furthest a client sees ships (`SHIP_VIEW_OUT`) plus margin, so ships are live before they are visible.
- A system's memory is restored before a pilot arrives (gate mouth, `MEMORY_GATE_MOUTH`); each spawner keeps its own respawn clock, in world ticks (server tick plus offset) so downtime counts.
- memory.json is written whole and durable (`writeFileDurable`); an unreadable file is moved to `.bad-<time>` and the world starts fresh. Bump `MEMORY_VERSION` on a format change.
- Hazards act only server-side (nothing moves a ship or changes its battery) so prediction is unaffected; warmth reaches clients as a whole percent (snapshot data[13]) and both sides derive the stage from it.
- Warmth must NOT couple to ship heat or thermal power yet (energy-system.md B8 plans it; do not add it here). The "heat" in Warmth.ts is environmental (stars, stations, fires, lava worlds).
- Every new data file or field gets a validator in server/src/data/validateGameData.ts.

## How to extend
1. **Add a system**: entry in data/systems.json (copy a neighbour; `portals` both ways, `stations`, `nests`, `hazards`, `traffic`, `derelicts`); ids referenced in factions.json `spawns` and quests. Run the data validator test (any game test) and `server/src/travel.test.ts`.
2. **Add a hazard**: add to `HAZARDS` (hazards.ts), tolerance/rules in data/hazards.json, material ratings in materials.json, effect in server Hazards.ts, client toast text via strings. Cold is separate (Warmth).
3. **Add a warmth source/stage**: heat `sources` in hazards.json `warmth`; stage tables in common/src/ships/warmth.ts (client and server read the same). Test warmth.test.ts (both common and server).
4. **Add a traffic route / derelict**: `traffic` (blueprint, route of station ids) or `derelicts` on the system; logic in GameServer `trafficSystem`. Add a memory case only for new spawner kinds (SystemMemory.ts + `MemoryTimers`).
5. **Add a new spawner kind that persists**: give it a capture/restore pair, a clock in `MemoryTimers`, and bump `MEMORY_VERSION` if the file shape changes.
6. **Add an `Intent`** for a distant ship: new variant in SimLod.ts `Intent`, its leg maths in farRoute.ts (shared with client FarShips, mirror there).
7. **Add a surface/arena/pocket**: system entry with `surface` / `arena` / `pocket`; Surfaces.ts / Arenas.ts / pockets.ts for behaviour.

## Gotchas
- hazards.json `_comment` says ratings live in `systems.json hazardRatings`; the real field is each system's `hazards` array. Trust the schema.
- A boss or pack that never goes distant usually has a `busy`/`sleepBlocker` reason; check `ShipBridge.sleepBlocker` first.
- Sleep is also blocked by anything "watched" (a pilot's selected target).
- Eviction happens ~20 s (`evictAfterSeconds`) after the last pilot leaves; tests that hop a gate and back must account for it.
- `data/world/memory.json` is runtime state: do not commit or ship; the VPS keeps its own.
- Sieges.ts (1001 lines) and GameServer.ts are god-files: grep, read slices.

## Notes (moved from DEVELOPMENT.md and the old engine-map)
- **Cold and warmth**: in a system with a `cryo` rating, cold builds up on a pilot's ship away from heat (stars, stations, ships on fire, lava worlds), slower through insulating materials (`materials.json insulation`). Numbers: `data/hazards.json warmth`; stages (guns slower, then a numb drive, then a seize): `common/src/ships/warmth.ts`; server meter `server/src/game/Warmth.ts` and `ShipBridge.warm`, where frost hits (gemmed guns, Frostbite Lance, Brood Mother) also take warmth. The pilot gets it as snapshot `data[13]` (percent) and sees the frost vignette (`client/src/ui/FrostVignette.ts`; a tint only on Low quality).
- **The world's memory** (`server/src/game/SystemMemory.ts`): a system nobody is in is not simulated; after `rules.json memory.evictAfterSeconds` it is written down and removed, and brought back (moved on, mended a little) as a pilot arrives, so a boss you killed or a nest you cleared stays so until due back, through restarts too. Kept in `data/world/memory.json` (gitignored, never overwritten by a deploy; delete `data/world/` with the server stopped for a fresh world). Knobs under `memory`: `evictAfterSeconds`, `healPerMinute` (share of each part's max HP mended a minute away), `bossHealPerMinute`, `fastForwardMinutes`, `saveSeconds`. What is saved: packs its own spawners sent (`Pack.origin`; events' and jobs' packs just go), bosses (hull, phases), freighters, derelicts, and every spawner's clock (boss `nextSpawn`, nests' `nestNext`/`nestRebuilt`/`nestLock`, zones', roaming packs', freighters', derelicts' respawns). Arrival (at a gate or rift mouth, `MEMORY_GATE_MOUTH`, or any way) brings it all back first: each ship flown on from where it was (`lod.adopt`, at most `fastForwardMinutes`), a boss's phases as its hull now is; wrecks do not mend; NPC ships carry no ammunition. File format `MemoryFile`, `MEMORY_VERSION` 1: `{ version, savedAt, worldTick, timers: { enemies: {...}, bosses }, systems: { <id>: { at, packs, bosses, traffic, derelicts } } }` in world ticks (server tick plus `memory.offset`, which carries the clock over restarts: downtime counts); written whole (`.tmp` then rename) every `saveSeconds` and at shutdown (`saveAll`); a missing or other-version file is a fresh world, an unparseable one is kept as `memory.json.bad-<time>`.
- **Distant ships** (`server/src/ships/SimLod.ts`): a pilotless ship no pilot has been within 64 km of for 10 s goes distant (never while busy), and wakes the tick one comes within 52 km (a jump's whole reach counts while it spools). A distant ship flies a route worked out by time, a leg at a time (`common/src/ships/farRoute.ts`: flight's integrator with the thrust held, closed form), each leg planned once when the last ends from its intent (`Enemies.intent`, `GameServer.trafficIntent`, or its autopilot) with its own dice. Where it is gets worked out only when asked (`lod.where`, the map, a look for pilots nearing, at most 10 s apart and at once where someone has just arrived). A patrol round a stationary home (`Intent` `patrol`) is worked out leg by leg without the AI (`patrolNext`), the same on the client.
- **The map's far ships** (`mapDots`): once a second while the map is open, only what changed: a leg (`dots`) or patrol (`routes`) per ship, carried on by the client (`client/src/state/FarShips.ts`), `info` once, `gone`, `full` for a fresh list; other pilots are on it too (kind 4).
- **Sectors and rifts**: Cinder Reach (red giant, slag belt, Ember Forge), The Maw (black hole), Glasswater Expanse (pulsar, Prism Relay); gates link sol - cinder_reach - the_maw and borealis - glasswater; The Void Sector connects through the Maw; Aurora Rift pair between Borealis and Nebula Prime; Cyan Rift Sol-Glasswater. `rifts` in systems.json (`RiftData`): a `Portal` with `kind = 1` pulls in any player within 200 units and fast-travels them keeping momentum (3 s cooldown). A shared `travelTo` serves gates, rifts and admin teleport; gates land you beside the gate that leads back. Debris and ship contacts stay within their own system.
- **Planets, comets, structures, stillstones, derelicts** (all in systems.json): `planets` (`PlanetData`: kind, radius, slow orbit, rings, moons; positions from the tick via `common/src/world/planets.ts`; scenery you fly over); `comets` (Kepler orbit, `cometPosition`; Halvorsen); `structures` (`solar_ring`, the Helios Array); `lodestones` (stillstones: orbit or fixed `position`, `size`, `state` active/scarred/dead/shattered, a scarred one's `scar` arc, optional camp `zone`; maths in flight guide); `derelicts` (`DerelictData`: spawned at start as battered pilotless wrecks via `ShipBridge.spawnWreck`, seeded damage, drift without gravity; whoever lands the last shot on a core gets the salvage, credits plus a quarter as XP, via `ShipHost.onShipDestroyed`; return after 5 minutes). Freighter traffic (`traffic`): three Ceres Haulers fly station loops. Sol also has Ares and Nereid, Lagrange Shipyard, Sentinel Watch, the Vesta Graveyard and Colony Ship Perseverance. `routePlan.ts` never detours through dead or shattered stillstones.
- **Asteroids**: orbits run 3x slower (`ORBIT_MU` 625000/9; station orbit speed is capped by `MAX_STATION_ORBIT_SPEED`). Spin is steady by id (`asteroidSpin(id, tick)` in `common/src/asteroid/polygon.ts`), applied to the hit outline on the server and to the drawn rock and mining-beam check on the client. Orbit timetable and the per-tick collision structures are in the server-core notes.
- **Gravity and hazards**: wells (`centralBody.gravity`: strength, minRadius, horizon) are in the shared flight step; `systemInfo` carries `hazards`; `client/src/ui/HazardOverlay.ts` adds haze, darkness, radiation, cold, warning chips and an event-horizon alert that scales with your thrust. Hazard `_comment` mismatch: see Gotchas.
- **Planet surfaces** (`server/src/game/Surfaces.ts`): a surface is a systems.json entry with `surface` (`of`, `planet`, `kind`, `radius`, `seed`, optional `city`, `siege`); its planet names it (`planets[].surface`). A small bounded system where everything works as in space. X in orbit within 900 of the planet's edge descends (`GameServer.handleInteract` -> `Surfaces.descend`), arriving on the side you came from; past `radius` you climb back beside the planet. A city with a `siege` raises shield pylons (`city_pylon`, a thrusterless ward) and every `everySeconds` lays siege: 20 s warning, then `waves` from the edge for the nearest standing pylon; hold with a pylon left and everyone who fought near the city is paid, lose them all and the shields fall. Progress goes out as `objective`/`objectiveEnd` (banner `client/src/ui/ObjectiveBanner.ts`; admin `/event siege`). Terra Nova (Sol, city New Meridian, no siege configured) is the first. Planets, moons and comets are placed from the continuous clock (`GameState.getRawServerTick`) so they glide.
- **Moon sieges** (`server/src/game/Sieges.ts`): a moon can have a surface (`planets[].moons[].surface`, `surface.moon`); with a `bastion` everyone gets their own copy (a party shares one): `instance.slots` are extra system numbers for the same system (`GameData.getSystemInstances`), clients are told only its id. X over the moon (`Surfaces.descend` -> `Sieges.enter`) takes a pilot or their party to the copy, made on first arrival with the bastion (`siege_bastion`, a ward) on its crater. By day the party spends shared supplies (`SIEGE_SHOP`: patch the bastion and turrets, build `SIEGE_TURRETS` (cannon, flak, laser, missile; marks I-III) on six pads, scrap one for half back), anyone can refit, everyone readies up or claims their haul (`{ type: 'siege', action, pad? }`). Structures are pinned (`ShipBridge.pin`). Dusk 6 s, then night: `waveRoster` spends a budget that grows each night and with party size, leaning on whatever unlocked most recently (first four nights slow Scrapborn; pirates from night 5, cinder and frost from 8-9, wraiths from 12, the Maw from 19), one front or two, a champion every fifth night. Dawn adds to each pilot's haul (paid on claiming by day, `haulBonus` +25% per five nights, or half with no bonus if they leave otherwise or the bastion falls; `Sieges.leave` lifts them to orbit). A copy nobody is on is cleared after a minute. Client: `ui/SiegePanel.ts`, the `objective` banner (`night` darkens the ground), `rendering/surface/BastionView.ts`. Siege moons per grade: Selene (Sol, Mk I), Rimeholt (Borealis, II), Cinderhold (Cinder Reach, III), Gloam (the Void, IV), Last Light (the Maw, V); `bastion.grade` sets the nights' grade and pay (bastion and turrets one grade above); `bastion.offset` exists but is 0 everywhere. `GameServer.threats()` sends every system's and siege moon's rating in `welcome`; the client shows it in the HUD (`ui/threat.ts`, easy/fair/hard/deadly), maps, target panel and hangar summary.
- **Locked sectors**: `restricted` systems name a shared `licence` (Glasswater, Borealis, Nebula Prime and Cinder Reach need `outer_lanes` from Okafor's Cleared for Departure); gates and rifts refuse unlicensed ships; `SystemMapUI.locked` draws a padlock.
