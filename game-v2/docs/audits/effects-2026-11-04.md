# Effects, not sources: other ships' causes (2026-11-04)

Stage: "Effects" of the netcode work (docs/design/awareness-model.md 6.1 and phase 5; docs/design/confirmed-hits.md G7).
Built: `CAPS.CAUSES` (2048, protocol 39), on the dev server and in dev clients (`?causes=0|1`). How it works:
docs/areas/networking/causes-waypoints-pilots.md, "Other ships' causes".

## What changed for a client with the cap

- A contact between two ships, a rebuild's recentring and a pilot's pull reach it as cause records on the ships they
  moved. It works them out on its tick layer with the server's own function, so those ships are no longer keyframed.
- It is sent a keyframe of a ship it holds only when its replica can't be worked out from what it has. That covers a
  ship rebuilt twice in one window, a contact with a ship whose structure it lacks, or a check that came back wrong.
- Its own check adds a byte over every ship it holds. If the byte is off, the server keyframes them all again.
- Legacy clients, and clients without the cap, get exactly what they got before.

- **What moved other ships:** an attribution pass over the bridge's phases on the old tree found contacts among AI ships
  (Scrap Mites grinding), then the boss's contacts and drive, then rebuild shifts. With the cap, every one of those
  keyframes is gone apart from the few listed in the table.
- **Contact records:** a contact is one record for both ships, about 2-3 bytes packed. It carries neither side's state

- **Hits:** no fixes and no hit-chain mismatches in any scenario. Hits on a ship a record names are told for that
  window, as they are for a keyframed ship.

## Bugs found and fixed on the way

- **Contact with a dying ship:** the other ship was removed in the same snapshot, so its structure was gone by the time
  the replica stepped. Its structure is now kept from when the snapshot came.
- **Own-ship contact state:** an own-ship contact row went without the other ship's state even when that ship was
  keyframed later in the same snapshot. This happened when the other was new to the client or one it couldn't work out.
  It was the crowd boss fight's 5,800 disagreements, and possibly the old self "2".
- **Rebuild between unknown structures:** a rebuild cause went out when the client couldn't have the structures on either
  side, for example after a death's uncaused rebuild. That case is now a keyframe (`rebuildOk`).

## Known gaps

- **Shells entering the focus:** not built. They still come as rounded rows with their hits told. Shell spawn
  quantisation, arrivals and inflow blocks are still to do.
- **Debris (357 ticks off a run in the debris probe, preexisting):**
  - Some pieces jump about half a unit in one tick with no knock, no rebuild of their own and no `touched`. Nothing
    keyframes them.
  - A destroyed ship's flung pieces derive wrong. The ack catches it and they are keyframed a few ticks later (the boss
    probe's 5).
- **Contacts in grinding packs:** each pair-tick is about 2-3 bytes. A "same as last tick" form, or client-side contact
  detection (pairs in id order, a golden shift), would cut that further.
- **Pulls on pilotless ships:** these are not rounded, so they still keyframe. Pulls on AI ships come from the boss
  magnet, which is waiting on boss decisions being inputs.
