# The Heart of the Maw, redesigned: look, stages and fight

Status: **design only** (2026-11-04). Nothing here is built, and nothing in this work changes how the game runs. The live
boss is still `boss_maw_heart` in `data/bosses.json` (design `maw_heart_titan`: a grid ship with nova and escort
phases). Its new look is generated by `scripts/bespoke/maw_heart.mjs` into `docs/bosses/maw-heart/`, which the game does
not load. Stills are rendered by `scripts/bespoke/maw_heart_render.mjs`, which runs a private copy of the native
renderer from a mirror folder outside the tree, so no file ever enters `data/`. The stills are in
`docs/panels/2026-11-04/bosses/maw-heart/`.

Follows `docs/boss-design.md`. The model for this document is `docs/bosses/brood-mother.md`. **NEW** marks work that
does not exist yet. Numbers are starting values for the audit in section 6. Ticks are 1/60 s.

## 1. Identity

One line: **an outcrop of the shard ring that beats, and pulls.** The other bosses are fought by learning a move order.
This one is fought by learning a **rhythm**. Every signature move, every damage window and every per-pilot attack lands
on its beat. Its pressure is gravity: it draws you in.

| | Pyreclast | Brood Mother | Heart of the Maw |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | a molten engine of turrets | a living nursery | **a rooted, crusted thing of dark rock round a knot of glass, buried in a bed of rubble** |
| Core verbs | barrage, broadside, lance, vent | spawn, tether, chill | **beat, pull, calve** |
| Staging | outer to inner layers (plating, body, heart) | fins, mantle, clutch | **round the beat: one sealed chamber opens at a time, clockwise, then the chambers in pairs, then the knot alone** |
| How pieces go | shot off and blown away outermost first | shot off, wrecks, blown away | **crust calves and falls toward the black hole; roots come away whole at their knot; chambers gape and then burst one per beat; it dies by imploding** |
| Damage window | vent (150%) | hatch (150%) | **diastole: the open chamber or pair, 150% up to a cap per pilot, 25% elsewhere** |
| Gear meta | part-breaking, heat, separate power | thermal against ice, turrets against swarms | **fly light: thrust-to-mass to beat the pull, and burst weapons to fill short windows** |
| Glow | red-orange | violet | **disk gold** (the accretion disk's light, `#ffc24a`; no other boss uses it) |

It does not travel. It is anchored in its bed and only turns, slowly. That is new for a staged boss. The arena moves
round it instead: pilots must reposition to reach the open chamber.

## 2. Look and silhouette

![The stages](../panels/2026-11-04/bosses/maw-heart/maw-heart.png)

Generator: `scripts/bespoke/maw_heart.mjs` (61 parts, plus 39 render-only boulders for the dormant still). It is
four-fold about its middle (the four axes), mirrored left to right, with **two deliberate breaks**: a crown of glass on
the apex only, and longer roots behind (the side it lies buried on). So it reads as one thing with a front and a back,
not as a wheel.

**Second pass (2026-11-04, after Kieran's "a bit chaotic and flat").** The first pass had ten roots and seven crown
shards of uneven length, crust in many same-sized slabs, and gold only as thin slivers. The outline read as noise. The
refined look is built in three sizes:
- **Big masses** that set the silhouette: four caps, four buttresses, four roots, the crown.
- **A middle layer** of structure: stepped plates (each cap is three stepped faces with a lit rim), the knots, and the
  chamber walls.
- **Fine detail** only where the eye should go: the four gold seams. Each is a wide channel with a hot core line and
  warm-lit stone either side, so the light reads as falling on the rock. The seams converge on the **eye** at the
  middle, where the core glows through an iris in the keystone.

Depth comes from layering:
- **Overlap and relief.** Caps lie over the buttresses, every plate casts a shadow and has a side wall (`plate` relief),
  and recesses are darker than faces, which are darker than rims.
- **The throats.** The chambers' throats are sunk and dark, with light at the bottom.
- **Contrast between layers.** The pale bone of the lattice under the crust stands against the dark stone and the gold.
- **The bed.** The rubble is now a crater rim of graded boulders (big on the diagonals, small between, a gap ahead) and
  a drift over its back, so the Heart is clearly the subject.
- The first pass is kept for comparison: `maw-heart-v1-vs-v2.png` and `v1-maw-heart.png` in the panels folder.

- **Size.** Its roots reach **~2460 world units** from its middle and its crust **~1470**. The live Heart is about 640
  (`maw-heart-before.png` and `maw-heart-after.png` are drawn at the same zoom). The Brood Mother is ~1600, the Scrap
  Mother ~1365 and the Pyreclast ~1135, so this is the largest boss by body, and by reach after the Brood Mother's arms.
- **Silhouette at a glance.** A round, stepped mass with a gold cross through it and a glowing eye at the middle, a
  crown of three glass shards on its apex, two short roots sweeping out ahead, and two long roots curling back behind.
  The cross echoes the live boss's phase line: "four channels in, along its axes".
- **Stage 1, the bed** (34 parts):
  - four **caps** on the axes, which seal the four chambers (`ruin`: these decide the stage);
  - four **buttresses** on the diagonals, each inner and outer (8 pieces), and a **keystone** over the middle with the
    eye in it;
  - a crown of three glass shards;
  - **four roots**, each held by a glowing **knot** at its base, with three tapering lengths;
  - the **lattice** under the crust: its skeleton, pale bone veins that fork the same way at every scale, with a gold
    vein in the inner ring. It is bared as the crust calves away.
- **Stage 2, the chambers** (17 parts): apex, two flanks and base. Each has two dark-metal walls with lit rims, and a
  **valve lip** whose throat is sunk deep and dark with light glowing at its bottom. A **septum** with an iris sits over
  the middle. These are all `ruin`: worn out, they gape, they don't fall. Four glass **arteries** run on the diagonals
  between the chambers (optional: they feed its pull).
- **Stage 3, the node** (10 parts, `under`): a **core** of seam glass lit from within (a dark iris round a gold sphere,
  white-hot at its middle) in a ring of dark metal; four **valve teeth** on the diagonals (`ruin`: they and the core
  decide the fight); four **stubs** on the axes (optional).
- **Connectivity**: the generator checks that every part reaches the core through overlapping parts: in full, with stage
  1 gone, and with stages 1 and 2 gone. All three pass. The real check (`rebuildShip` `orphanGroups`) is in the build
  list.

## 3. Stage layout: the beat

**The idea.** The Pyreclast is peeled from outside in. The Mother is fought rack, then nest, then mill. The Heart is
fought **round its beat**. In every stage the same clock opens part of it and closes the rest. Each stage changes
**what** opens and **how often**:

| | Stage 1, the bed | Stage 2, the chambers | Stage 3, the node |
|---|---|---|---|
| What opens on the beat | **one cap at a time, clockwise**: apex, right flank, base, left flank | **opposite pairs** in turn: apex + base, then the two flanks | **the whole node**, every beat |
| Beat period | 8 s | 7 s, **0.5 s shorter for each chamber wrecked** (arrhythmia; at least 5 s) | 6 s (4.8 s enraged) |
| Window (diastole) | 3 s on the open cap | 2.5 s on the open pair | 2 s, **+0.5 s for each valve tooth wrecked** (it can't close fully) |
| Beaten by | the four caps (`ruin`) | the chambers and septum (`ruin`) | the four valve teeth and the core (`ruin`) |
| Optional parts, and what breaking them does | roots (cut at the knot: the whole root comes away), buttresses, keystone, crown | arteries | stubs |
| Its movement | turns to keep the **closed** caps toward its target (it hides the window) | turns to put an axis on its target | still; it never turns |

So stage 1 makes you **fly round it** to reach the open cap. Stage 2 lets you choose which open chamber to work on, and
every one you wreck **speeds the beat up and takes a move away**. Stage 3 is a fast metronome round a small target.

**How the pieces break (distinctive to it):**

1. **Calving (stage 1).** Crust and root pieces shot off don't fly outward. They **fall toward the black hole**:
   `shed` with the fling along the bearing from the lair to the system's middle, `crumble` 150. Across the arena, all
   loose rock streams one way, down the well. It is readable, and no other boss does it.
2. **Cut at the knot (stage 1).** Each root holds to the crust only through its knot. Shoot the knot and the whole root
   (three lengths) comes away in one piece, calving as above. This uses the connectivity rule the game already has: what
   no longer reaches the core detaches. A side-mount or turret build can sever a root with one burst instead of
   chewing three segments.
3. **Gape (stage 2).** A worn-out chamber piece stays on as a wreck with its lip lit and open. Shots into a gaping
   chamber pass on **in full** to the nearest whole piece (the existing wreck rule), so a gape is a doorway to the next
   chamber.
4. **Burst on the beat (transitions).** Stage 1 to 2: on the next beat **all four caps and the remaining crust blow at
   once**, the lattice last, half a second later. Stage 2 to 3: the chambers burst **one per beat, in the order they
   opened**, on four quick beats (1 s apart). That is 4 s, not the Pyreclast's outermost-first chain.
5. **Implosion (death).** A 1.5 s shudder. Then every remaining piece is pulled **inward** to the core (a negative
   fling) and crumbles. The light goes out, and the boss is gone.

## 4. The fight

### 4.1 Rules that hold in every stage

- **Weak-point windows.** In diastole, the open deciding parts take **150%**, up to a **cap per pilot per window**:
  1500 in stages 1 and 2, 1200 in stage 3. Past the cap that pilot's hits drop to 25%. Outside windows, deciding parts
  take **25%**. Optional parts (roots, buttresses, keystone, crown, arteries, stubs) always take **100%**. So some damage lands
  outside windows, and part-breaking is never wasted.
- **Signature moves.** Shared, **one at a time**, never scaled with pilot count. Every signature lands on a beat and is
  followed by **a rest beat**: a full beat with no signature, window open as usual. Each has **one dodge rule that
  always works**. Tells are light: a change in the gold light of the part that does it, plus a thin telegraph for the
  last ~1.2 s. Every move in the same family uses the same tell (all pulls dim the seams; every chamber move swells its
  lip).
- **Per-pilot attacks.** On every beat that has no signature landing, **each pilot within 6000 rolls a fixed chance**
  (stage 1: 30%, stage 2: 25%, stage 3: 35%). A hit puts one local, avoidable attack on that pilot. The roll is pure
  luck: there is no aggro or damage ledger behind it. Never more than one per pilot at a time. Others may catch it.
- **No bullet hell.** The most projectiles from one move is 16 (the stage-3 ring). Shots are few and big.
- **Bite.** A signature taken in full costs about **35–45% of a level-26 combat hull**; a per-pilot attack about
  **15–20%**. Two mistakes in a row are survivable; three are not. Tune by section 6's audit.

### 4.2 Stage 1, the bed (100% to 66.7%)

Loop (one signature, then a rest beat): **Calve → Inhale → Root lash** (6 beats, ~48 s).

| Move | Tell | Effect | Dodge rule (always works) | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Calve** | the open cap's seam turns from gold to white for 1.2 s; dust streams off its rim | three big crust slabs thrown straight out of the open cap, along its axis, in a 50° cone to 3200 | **Leave the open cap's face when its seam goes white.** | as usual, on that cap: the greedy spot is the dangerous one; shoot it from the cone's edge |
| **Inhale** | all four seams go dark together; the roots draw in (1.5 s) | 2 s pull toward it: **0.55 units a tick at 1500, falling to 0 at 4500**; then the crust bites (a ring strike from its hull to 1700) | **Thrust straight away when the seams go dark.** | none on this beat; the next window is 4 s instead of 3 |
| **Root lash** | the knots on one side brighten one by one, root to tip (1.2 s) | it turns 70° that way in 1.4 s; each standing root sweeps its arc (strike lanes over the swept area) | **Be inside the crust's edge or past the root tips, never between.** | as usual |

- **Per-pilot: shard fall** (30% a beat). A faint ring (radius 380) under the pilot for 1.2 s. Then three rubble shards
  fall into it **from the Maw's side**, the same direction the calving falls. Leave the ring.
- **Breakage:** each root cut removes its sweep from Root lash (gaps you made yourself). All roots on a side gone means
  no lash that way. Calve needs the buttresses beside the open cap: with both gone, that cap calves one slab instead
  of three. Each crown shard shot off shortens Inhale's bite ring by 100.

### 4.3 Stage 2, the chambers (66.7% to 33.4%)

Each chamber **owns one move**. The loop runs clockwise from the apex, skipping wrecked chambers: **Spit (apex) →
Spill (right flank) → Undertow (base) → Spill (left flank)**, each followed by a rest beat. Wreck a chamber and its move
is gone from the loop, and the beat is 0.5 s faster. Fewer moves, faster rhythm, shorter gaps: the stage changes as you
break it.

| Move | Tell (the family tell: its lip swells and brightens, 1.0 s) | Effect | Dodge rule | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Spit** (apex) | apex lip swells | five big slow glass orbs in a tight fan **along the apex's axis** (fixed to the body; the Heart turns slowly) | **Never be on a chamber's axis when its lip swells.** | the opposite pair is open, so the safe spot is the paying one |
| **Spill** (flanks) | that flank's lip swells | pours a band of molten seam that sweeps 90° from the flank's axis **toward the apex**, pooling for 4 s (burn) | **When a flank swells, go aft of it.** | as usual |
| **Undertow** (base) | base lip swells; the arena light dims a touch | a well under the target (radius 1100): pull 0.5 units a tick toward its middle for 2.5 s, then it collapses (strike, radius 350, at the middle). Strength scales with arteries standing (0 to 4: x0.4 to x1) | **Leave the circle before it closes.** | as usual |

- **Per-pilot: vein tap** (25% a beat). A thin gold line from the nearest standing artery to the pilot for 1 s. Then a
  lance down the line, frozen where it was drawn. Move off the line. **Each artery cut removes its taps.** With all four
  gone there are no taps, and Undertow is at x0.4.

### 4.4 Stage 3, the node (33.4% to 0): the hardest

It never turns. The node is small (radius ~500), the beat is fast, and the whole node opens on every beat. Loop:
**Collapse → Lens → Flatline**, each with a rest beat.

| Move | Tell | Effect | Dodge rule | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Collapse** | the node's light draws inward (its glow shrinks) for 1.5 s | pull 0.6 units a tick within 3500 for 1.5 s, then an implosion ring out to 1600 | **Thrust away the moment the light draws in.** (Inhale's family) | none this beat; the next is +1 s |
| **Lens** | a thin gold ring at radius 900 round it; its brightest standing stub glows | 16 big slow orbs expand from 900 to 4000 over 4 s, with **a gap of three orbs facing the brightest stub** | **Go through the gap at the bright stub.** Each stub shot off adds a second, third... gap | as usual |
| **Flatline** | the beat stops: no light, no window for two beats (the silence) | then three quick beats, each a shock ring from radius 600 outward | **Hug the node when it falls silent.** (The rings start outside 600.) | the rest beat after has a 3 s window: the reward for holding your nerve |

- **Per-pilot: fall** (35% a beat). A lensing mark where the pilot would be in 1.5 s flying straight. Then a small
  singularity there for 1 s (pull radius 500, then damage). Change course. This is a callback to the live boss's
  singularity nova.
- **Breakage:** each valve tooth wrecked lengthens every window by 0.5 s. Each stub shot off adds a Lens gap.
- **Enrage at 15%** (`phases` 4th entry, `enrage: 1.3`): beat 6 s to 4.8 s, tells x0.85 (never under 0.8 s).

### 4.5 Fight length: the maths

Source: `docs/audits/weapons-balance-2026-11-01.md`, "After the suggested numbers" (sustained DPS, one gun, full
power, every shot landing). **Assumption** (open question 1): a level-26 combat build carries about ten guns from its
band (Coilgun 89, Stinger 101, Overpulse 90, Phase 82, Cutter 120, Eel 125: about 95 average), so **~1000 paper DPS**.

Effective DPS on the boss at multiplier 1, `E` = paper x **0.85** (penetration and resist against grade-5 boss
material; the audit's x0.775 to x1.15 range) x **accuracy** x **0.75 uptime** (dodging and repositioning). At realistic
accuracy **0.6**: **E ≈ 380**.

Damage on deciding parts per beat, for one pilot: `min(1.5 · E · window, cap) + 0.25 · E · (beat − window)`.

| | Stage 1 | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beat / window / cap | 8 s / 3 s / 1500 | 7 s / 2.5 s / 1500 | 6 s / 2.5 s average / 1200 |
| Per beat at E = 380 | min(1710, 1500) + 475 = 1975 | 1425 + 428 = 1853 | min(1425, 1200) + 333 = 1533 |
| Average DPS on deciding parts | 247 | 265 | 256 |
| Target time | 3.5 min | 3.5 min | 2.5 min |
| **Deciding HP (= time x DPS)** | **52k** (4 caps x 13k) | **56k** (4 chambers x 13k + septum 4k) | **38k** (4 teeth x 6.5k + core 12k) |

Activation 6 s, the stage 1→2 burst 3 s, the 2→3 bursts 4 s: about 13 s with nothing to hit, plus Inhale's and
Flatline's withheld windows (already inside the averages).

| Accuracy | E | Stage times (s) | **Total, solo** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.45 (poor) | 287 | 252 / 280 / 172 | **~12.1 min** |
| **0.6 (realistic)** | 382 | 210 / 210 / 150 | **~9.9 min** |
| 0.75 (good) | 478 | 198 / 192 / 141 | **~9.2 min** |

The caps squeeze the range from the top: better aim saves under a minute. The fight lands inside 8–12 minutes for most
pilots, and no build can burst it down. Optional parts (roots ~4k each, buttresses ~2k each, keystone 3k, crown
~1k a shard, arteries 1.5k, stubs 1k: about 48k in all) are on top. They take time, but they buy safety.

**In a group** (no HP scaling, caps per pilot): three pilots of the same kind deal about 3x, so ~3.5–4 minutes. The
signatures stay the same, and the per-pilot attacks scale with presence (each pilot carries their own). Open question
2 asks whether a big group needs a floor.

`bosses.json` `hp` is the knob that brings piece HP to these totals once the materials exist. Its stage health is not
equal in time but is equal in the bar (`stagedIntegrity`), as for every staged boss.

### 4.6 The gear meta and how the game hints it

**The meta: fly light, hit in the gap.**
- **Thrust-to-mass** beats its pulls. Inhale, Undertow, Collapse and fall all push velocity (the existing `pull`: the
  same push for every ship). Only a ship whose acceleration beats ~0.55–0.6 units a tick escapes in time. A heavy
  armour brick (stacked plate, and leestone plate above all: "Ark-heavy") is dragged into the bite. Drives over plate.
- **Burst weapons and stored energy** fill the short, capped windows. Clip and charge guns (Stinger, Overpulse,
  Longbarrel, Spear, Rail Driver) and capacitor banks reach a 1500 cap inside 2.5 s. A sustained-only build
  under-fills them.
- How it differs from the Pyreclast's meta (part-breaking, heat, separate power systems): here the build question is
  **mass against drive** and **burst against sustained**, not what to break or how to stay cool.

**The hints**, each with an owner:
- Warden Kask, at Horizon Watch, a new topic (after main_12): *"Past the shard ring, fly light. The Heart pulls, and the
  heavy ones don't come back. Strip your plate if you have to."*
- A Watch notice in its loss ledger (codex record, NEW): the crews it sent at the Heart, by hull; most of the lost
  were the heaviest. The archivist's note: "Heavy ships were sent at it first, because it's big."
- The den's **Sinker** (section 7) pulls too, so pilots learn the pull before they reach the Heart.
- The Heart's own Inhale tell (the seams going dark) is the first thing it does to a new pilot: the lesson is the
  first move.

## 5. Dormancy, activation, recovery, arena

**Dormant** (`maw-heart-dormant.png`). It lies half buried in its bed: rubble over its back half, its roots under it,
only the crown and the forward roots showing. The light is out except one faint pulse along the seams about every 16
s. It can't be hurt (shots hit the rubble). Pilots may fly round it.

**Activation** (6 s, invulnerable). Trigger: a pilot inside 3200, or a shot on its rubble.
1. 0 s: the intro card. Every receiver aboard fills with the heartbeat.
2. 1 s: the rubble slides off its back and **falls toward the black hole** (the calving direction, established early).
3. 2–4 s: the roots flex out one by one, clockwise; the four seams light in order: apex, right, base, left. That is the
   stage-1 window order, shown before it matters.
4. 5 s: the first full beat. A soft pull ring with no damage, a dust shockwave, and the arena barrier rises.

**Recovery.** With nobody in combat with it for **30 s**, it stops. Over **2 minutes** it restores fully, in reverse:
the stage resets to 1, broken pieces fade back in from the inside out, and rubble drifts back from the down-well side
onto its crust. Then it **returns to dormant**: the seams go dark one by one, the roots fold, and the bed settles.

**The arena.** An enclosed bowl in the shard ring round the lair (15302, -10594), 18.6k from the black hole and inside
the belt (14.8k–26.8k).
- **Barrier** (NEW, shared with the other reworked bosses): a ring at **5500** from the lair. Entry is free. Leaving
  takes **5 s out of combat** (no damage dealt or taken); until then the ring is solid to that pilot. Shots never cross
  it either way.
- **Inside:** the bed (rubble round the Heart, out to ~1800), and **six rubble pillars** at 3500–4800 (fixed rock,
  `fixedWalls`), cover against Calve and the Lens ring. The Heart's pulls are strongest near the middle, so the pillars
  are where heavy ships survive and where light ships fight from.
- The fall direction (toward the black hole, about bearing 235° from the lair) is the same every fight. Pilots can learn
  that "down" is where the debris goes.

## 7. Den: the approach, and its enemies

**The Rootway.** The only clear way into the bowl is a winding channel through the shard ring from its outer edge, about
8000 long. Rubble walls are fixed rock. There are three dens along it:
1. **The Spill** (outer end): open water of rubble, Shardling packs.
2. **The Narrows** (middle): one Sinker holding the tightest pass, two Calves.
3. **The Lip** (the bowl's edge): Calves on the rim, where the barrier starts.

Den enemies are small bespoke designs with breakable pieces (a generator each, like this one), flown by the AI pilot
body (`common/src/bodies/aiPilot.ts`, `CAPS.PILOTS`). They feel the Heart's beat: they act on it.

| Enemy (pilots' names) | Look and size | Pieces you can break | Behaviour | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Calf** | a calved crust slab, radius ~150, two root-stub legs, a gold knot | 2 legs (no legs: it can't ram), 3 crust plates, the knot (dies) | drifts, then **rams on the beat** (telegraphed by its knot flaring) | the beat; cutting what attacks |
| **Sinker** | a crusted ball, radius ~260, slow, a knot of seam glass under three plates | 3 plates (bare the knot), the knot (its pull stops) | a **small well**: 0.3 units a tick within 900, always on; lobs one slab a beat | **thrust-to-mass**: light ships slip past, heavy ones get stuck |
| **Shardlings** | three crown-glass chips flying in a line, radius ~60 | a blade each (no blade: no damage) | fast strafing runs, fragile; burst on the beat | burst damage and turret tracking |

## 8. Lore text (every claim owned)

Rules (lore-canon.md and the public canon): narration says only what a pilot sees; every claim has an owner; nothing says
where the heartbeat comes from, what the Heart is, or that anything is alive or aware.

## 9. What it would take to build (brain, body and decision inputs)

Mapped to `docs/design/decision-inputs.md` (server brain, shared body, decisions on a 4-tick grid) and the modular
layout rule: a new boss adds a module and a registry line, never a branch in Bosses.ts, ShipBridge or GameServer.

**Data**
- [ ] Move the generator's output to `data/ships/bespoke/maw_heart.json` and `data/ships/blueprints/enemy/maw_heart.json`
      (switch the generator's `OUT`). Keep `maw_heart_titan` until the new one ships (guide pitfall).
- [ ] The four materials (section 2) in `materials.json` and `HULL_MAT` (`any: true`, `emit` gold); `npm run wire:sync`.
- [ ] `bosses.json`: `design: maw_heart`, `asDesigned`, `brain: maw_heart`, four phases (0.85 / 0.667 / 0.334 / 0.15

      along the Rootway, `systems.json` rubble walls and pillars (`surface.fixedWalls`).

**Brain (server only)** `server/src/game/brains/mawHeart.ts` on `StagedBrain`. It picks the loop moves (`MOVE`), rolls
the per-pilot attacks from its own RNG, runs the window cap ledger (damage authority stays on the server), and decides
dormancy, activation and recovery.

**Body (common, both sides)** `common/src/bodies/mawHeart.ts` plus a `registry.ts` line.
- **The beat clock is pure body.** Period and window come from stage, phase, wrecked chambers and valve teeth
  (structure at the tick, body rule 6) and the fight's start tick (in `SYNC`). So the beat costs **no decisions**: both
  sides know when it lands.
- Moves as explicit-step body modules (as 6.6 asks for the Brood Mother: no generators), each seeded by its `MOVE` seed.
- **Anchored flight** (NEW flight table): no translation, turn by `assist`. Stage 1 faces the closed side; stage 2 an
  axis on the target; stage 3 still.

**Decisions**
- `MOVE` (signatures), `STAGE`, `DIE`, `STATE`, `TARGET`: as they are.
- **NEW op `MARK`** (reserved slot 13): `pilot slot, kind (2), seed (8)`. One per per-pilot attack (about 3 bytes).
  The roll stays secret on the server; the attack's shape is body work from the seed.
- **NEW op or `STATE` bit: `DORMANT`/`WAKE`**, so a client runs the activation and the return to rest without a
  keyframe.

## 10. Open questions for Kieran

1. **Level-26 loadout.** The maths assumes ~10 guns at ~95 sustained (~1000 paper DPS). Is there a reference ship and
   loadout for level 26? HP scales straight with it.
2. **Groups.** Caps are per pilot, so six pilots kill it in about 2 minutes. Fine, or add a shared ceiling per window
   (for example, four pilots' worth)?
3. **Leestone.** Should its chamber walls look like, or assay as, leestone? It's a strong link between the Heart and
   the Ark's stone. The draft avoids saying so (stand-in material aside); the optional assay record echoes leestone's
   "grain looks worked" wording, which a player might connect.
4. **The lattice.** Its skeleton forks "the same way at every scale", the stillstones' signature in the public canon.
   Keep the visual echo, or make it plainly different?
5. **"Beats in time with the heartbeat."** The draft's intro only says the receivers fill and the light opens. Saying
   that its light pulses with the heartbeat is an observation a pilot could make, but it leans toward "it's the
   source". Allowed?
6. **Escorts.** The live fight has Lurker and Colossus escorts. The draft drops all adds from the fight and puts the
   den enemies outside it. Keep Maw Spawn in the den too?
7. **Anchored.** It never moves, only turns. OK for a stage-3 finale, or should the node break loose and drift in
   stage 3?
8. **Gold glow.** Disk gold `#ffc24a` beside the Pyreclast's red-orange: distinct enough in play?
9. **Death line.** "The receivers cut out, then the heartbeat comes back" said in the fight itself: OK, or keep that
   only in the codex record?
10. **Loot.** Keep the current table and `maw_fragment` at 3%, or give its stages drops (crown glass, seam glass) as
    materials for something Maw-themed?
