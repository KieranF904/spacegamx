## 2. Shared rules

### 2.1 Windows

A **window** is the recovery the boss shows after a signature move: claws jammed shut, an overshoot, a vent blowing, a
weapon cooling. During it:

- **Damage taken x2**, up to the window's **cap** (counted after armour and multipliers). The cap is shown as a meter under the
  boss bar that fills as damage lands, with a ring counting down the window's time.
- **Filling the cap early ends the window with a crack:** a bonus of **10% of the cap**, dealt at once as direct damage,
  and a **stagger** that keeps the boss still and harmless for the rest of the window's time. Damage during the stagger
  is taken at the outside rate. The stagger keeps the rest beat for everyone, so a group's loop is no shorter than a solo
  pilot's. Its reward is the bonus and a free few seconds, not a faster fight.
- **Outside windows, damage taken x0.35.** So a solo pilot always gets somewhere, and shooting the boss mid-move is
  never wasted, just slow.
- The **first 2 s of a window are a rest beat:** no per-pilot attack starts in them.
- Stage cover (`rules.stageCover` 0.4 for hits on a later stage's parts) still applies, before the window multiplier.
- These replace `taken()`'s braced 0.4 and open 1.5 for both bosses.
- **Caps are sized so a solo pilot fills about 2/3 of one,** and two or more fill it (3.1).

The rule lives once, in common (**NEW** `common/src/bosses/windows.ts`: `windowTaken(state, tick)` and cap accounting), so
the boss bar's meter, the hit ledger and the server agree tick for tick.

### 2.2 Per-pilot attacks

Every pilot present (inside the barrier) has opted in. There is no "engaged" tracking.

- **Rolls:** each beat (120 ticks), each pilot present rolls a fixed chance to draw a **personal attack**. It is pure luck:
  there is no fair rotation and no streak protection.
- **Damage and reach:** the attack is aimed at that pilot, local to them, dangerous and reasonably avoidable. Catching
  splash from someone else's personal attack is fine.
- **Loose cap:** at most `max(2, ceil(pilots / 2))` personal attacks run at once, never more than 6. It only matters in
  big groups. When the cap is full, a roll that wins is dropped, not queued.
- **Held off during a signature move:** none starts during a signature move's tell or active part, or in a window's
  rest beat. Ones already running finish.
- **Pool per stage:** each stage has 2 or 3 personal attacks, and the roll picks one with the same seeded roll.
- **Server decision:** a roll is `{slot, attack, seed}` stamped on the boss's decision channel: slot is the pilot's place
  in the boss's pilot list. The body plays it the same way on every client (section 11).
- **Tuning:** a bad streak (three landing in 10 s) takes about a quarter of the expected ship's HP. A pilot who dodges
  reasonably (lands 1 in 4) loses about 5-8% a minute to them.

| | Mother | Pyreclast |
|---|---|---|
| Chance per pilot per beat | 0.20 | 0.25 |
| Attacks per pilot per minute (eligible about 60% of the time) | about 3.6 | about 4.5 |
| One landing in full | about 90 (8% of a Sparrow T's 1,063 HP; under a third of its 300 HP core) | about 3% of a level 30 ship's HP (300 on a 10k ship) |

### 2.3 Signature moves

- **Shared and one at a time.** A signature move is never scaled by pilot count (no extra shots, no shorter rests, no
  bigger pulls). It targets the boss's current target, or the arena, as its rule says.
- **One dodge rule that always works,** stated in a few words in this doc and in the codex once a pilot has seen it.
  Nothing in a signature move is aimed where you are going, except when the rule is "change direction".
- **Rest beat:** every signature move is followed by its window, and the window opens with a 2 s rest beat.
- **Matched to the expected ship:**
  - **Mother (starter ships):** a Sparrow T tops out at 20.7 u/tick. Shots you dodge by moving across them fly at most
    12 u/tick (`scrap_chunk` is 11 today). Fast shots (rivets, 34) only travel down a line drawn before they fire.
  - **Pyreclast (level 30 ships):** they run about 25-30 u/tick. Free-flying shots fly at most 16 u/tick, and fast ones
    only travel down drawn lines.
- **Density cap:** a signature move puts at most **6 hostile shots within 800 u of any pilot** at once. Past that the
  brain drops shots from the pattern, deterministically, by the shot's order in its volley. That is still a server
  decision, so clients drop the same ones.
- **No filler fire:**
  - **Removed:** the Mother's between-move gun bursts (`IDLE_GUNS`) and flak (`IDLE_FLAK`), and the Pyreclast's resting
    turret balls.
  - **Kept:** the Pyreclast's vents burning anything close. They are the heat meta, not fire.

### 2.4 Tells

Tells are light and subtle, and they are always the same:

- **Kinds:** a turret swinging onto you, a part glowing, a sound, a puff of steam.
- **Warning time:** at least 0.9 s for a line attack and 1.2-1.5 s for an area one. Never shorter in later stages, and
  never shortened by enrage, which is removed.
- **Telegraph shapes:** the existing `bossTelegraph` shapes stay only where the attack's reach can't be read from the
  boss alone: a line, a landing circle. They are drawn **thin and faint** (about 35% of today's alpha). The tell itself
  is on the boss.
- **Consistency:** a tell never means two things. Each boss's table (sections 4 and 5) lists one tell per move, and no
  tell is reused across moves.

### 2.5 What the phases do now

`bosses.json` `phases` keep their `say` lines at the stage boundaries (0.667, 0.334), and the 0.85 brood line goes. All
`enrage` entries are removed. The "15%: coming apart" line stays as flavour only, with no change in speed.

## 3. Length: the maths

### 3.1 Why today's fights take hours

The deciding HP was measured from the real designs, with each part's armour and resists and the core x2.5 that
`Bosses.create` applies. The method was the same as in `docs/audits/weapons-balance-calc.ts`, as a throwaway script
that has since been deleted.

| Boss, stage | Deciding HP | Armour (share of HP) | What a starter Pip gets through | Pip-equivalent HP |
|---|--:|---|--:|--:|
| Mother 1 | 60,850 | 4 (84%), 3 | x0.077 | 792k |
| Mother 2 | 40,090 | 4 (72%), 3 | x0.085 | 472k |
| Mother 3 | 15,692 | 4 (58%), 3 | x0.094 | 167k |
| Pyreclast 1 | 108,912 | 5 (51%), 4.8, 4 | x0.18 at pen 2.5 | about 590k |
| Pyreclast 2 | 101,454 | 5 (41%), 4.8, 4 | x0.21 at pen 2.5 | about 485k |
| Pyreclast 3 | 45,088 | 5 (58%), 4 | x0.18 at pen 2.5 | about 256k |

- **Pip penetration:** a Pip is pen 0.5 (grade 1, autocannon kind -0.5). Against the Mother's scrap materials (grade 3,
  armour plates +1) the gap is -2.5 to -3.5, which the penetration table (`tuning.json damage.penetration`) turns
  into x0.05-x0.18.
- **Mother today:** a starter's two Pips (112 DPS sustained, `weapons-balance-2026-11-01.md`) take about 1.43M
  Pip-equivalent HP. Even with every shot landing, that is **3.5 hours**.
- **Pyreclast today:** at pen 2.5 (a typical level 30 Mk II-III gun), 260 raw DPS needs about **85 minutes at perfect
  aim**.

**So the rework fixes armour as well as HP.**

- **Mother (the generalist):** the starter boss's pieces must not shrug off the starter gun. Her `scrap_*` materials
  drop from grade 3 to **grade 1**, so her deciding pieces are armour 1. A Pip then gets x0.775 (penetration gap -0.5),
  times about 0.95 resist.
  - Those materials are also the Scrapborn den enemies' (Scrap Crawler, Rivet Sower). They are starter-area enemies, so
    grade 1 is right for them too.
  - Shot-off cover (hatches, booms) may keep an armour plate (armour 2).
- **Pyreclast (the meta boss):** its body (`pyre_glass`, the deciding pieces) drops from grade 4 to **grade 3**, armour 3.
  - **Limbs:** every limb that drives an attack (turrets, scythes, bays, mortars, lance, coils, vents) stays
    `pyre_chrome` / `pyre_steel` grade 4 with the armour function: **armour 5**.
  - **Body:** pen 2.5 against 3 gives x0.775, and pen 3.5-4 gives x1.075-1.15.
  - **Limbs:** pen 2.5 against 5 gives x0.175, and pen 4 gives x0.55. Breaking limbs is the meta's job (section 6).

Stage HP is set per stage by a **NEW** `bosses.json` field, `stageHp: [a, b, c]`. It rescales each stage's deciding parts
so they sum to that, with the core's share counted after its x2.5. The current `hp` scale stays for non-deciding parts.

### 3.2 The model

Per pilot, "landed DPS" is sustained DPS x penetration and resist x share of shots that hit x share of the time spent
firing.

| | Raw DPS | Pen x resist | During moves (hit x firing) | In windows (hit x firing) | Landed: moves / windows |
|---|--:|--:|---|---|--:|
| Starter vs Mother | 112 (2 Pips) | 0.775 x 0.95 | 0.5 x 0.6 | 0.85 x 0.95 | 24.7 / 66.5 |
| Level 30 off-meta vs Pyreclast | 260 | 0.775 | 0.45 x 0.45 (shared power: firing slows the dodge) | 0.8 x 0.95 (kept out of vent reach) | 40.8 / 153 |
| Level 30 built for it | 260 | 1.075 | 0.5 x 0.6 | 0.85 x 0.95, x1.1 burst from a Capacitor bank | 83.9 / 248 |

- **Starter:** 112 is the audit's Pip pair, 56 DPS sustained each.
- **Level 30:** 260 raw is a mid level 30 rig. The weapon-scenarios "Boss" case (25k HP, armour 3,
  `docs/design/weapon-scenarios.md`) gives Twinlink x5 187 effective DPS, Overpulse x5 142 and Slurry Cannon x3 317.
  260 raw at pen 2.5 (x0.775) is about 200 effective, between those.

A loop is the stage's signature moves, each made of: tell + active + 2 s repositioning (outside rate) + window.

- **Mother:** tell 1 s, active 5 s, window 7 s, so 15 s a move and **45 s a loop**. Every cap is 1,400.
- **Pyreclast stages 1-2:** tell 1.2 s, active 6 s, window 7 s, so 16.2 s a move and 48.6 s a loop. Cap 5,200.
- **Pyreclast stage 3:** three moves with 5 s windows (cap 3,640), then the vent with a 10 s window (cap 8,320), so 61.8 s
  a loop.

Overheads: the Mother's 6 s wake plus two 8 s stage blow-aways (22 s). The Pyreclast's 8 s wake plus two 10 s
blow-aways (28 s).

Window fill (solo):

- Starter in the Mother's window: 66.5 x 2 x 7 s = 931 of 1,400 = **0.67**.
- Built-for-it pilot in a Pyreclast window: 248 x 2 x 7 = 3,472 of 5,200 = **0.67**.
- Off-meta pilot: 0.41. Their windows are where they lose most time.

### 3.3 Results

The model is `docs/bosses/rework-2026-11-model.mjs` (`node docs/bosses/rework-2026-11-model.mjs`). Rerun it whenever a number above changes.

| Boss, who | Solo | 2 pilots | 3 pilots | 4 pilots | Target |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| Mother, starter (Sparrow T, 2 Pips) | **5.4 min** | 3.2 | 2.9 | 2.6 | 4-6 solo, 3-4 group |
| Pyreclast, level 30 off-meta | **9.9 min** | 5.2 | 3.8 | 3.6 | 8-12 solo |
| Pyreclast, level 30 built for it | **6.2 min** | 3.7 | 3.3 | 3.0 | 5-7 solo, faster in a group |

Per stage, solo:

- **Mother:** 90 s, 120 s and 90 s.
- **Pyreclast, built for it:** 101 s, 135 s and 107 s.
- **Stage split:** both bosses are 30/40/30 by HP. Stage 1 no longer holds most of it.

**Groups level off.** A full cap is 1.5x a solo fill plus the 10% crack. Only outside damage grows with every pilot, and
it is x0.35. That is why four pilots are not four times faster: the Mother goes 5.4 min solo, 2.6 min for four. A group
of four is a little under Kieran's "3-4 min". To slow groups further, lower the crack bonus or the outside multiplier
(0.3 gives 5.6 / 3.3 / 3.0 / 2.7). Do not raise HP per pilot.

**What the model leaves out, and the measuring pass must check:**

- **Off-meta limbs:** an off-meta pilot who never breaks limbs faces every attack, all fight long. The model's low
  move-time firing share for off-meta stands in for that.
- **Deaths:** none are counted, and in a public instance any death costs the dead pilot their run.
- **Ammo:** limits are ignored.

## 4. Scrap Mother

**Role:** the generalist starter boss. Any loadout works. There is no meta, no gear check and no part that must be broken.

**Expected ship:** Sparrow T and the first few buys (Hammer, Rattler, Glint, Lobber). About 20 u/tick top speed and
1,000-3,000 HP.

**Level:** 8 as today, so a level 3-8 pilot can try her.

### 4.1 Stages

| Stage | HP | Body | Habit | Signature moves (loop order) | Personal attacks |
|---|--:|---|---|---|---|
| 1, the hoard | 6,000 | nest wings, crane arms, grapple booms, brood racks | holds off at 1,800, turns slowly | rivet line, haul, crane sweep | marked rivets, mite pair |
| 2, the mill | 8,000 | body: throat, rotors, vats, stacks, drives | drives harder, closes to 1,200 | intake, shrapnel rings, charge | marked rivets, sludge drop |
| 3, the seed | 6,000 | heart: core, iris, servo ring, spikes, welders | small and quick; repositions once per loop, not before every move | scissor, spiral, seekers | sludge drop, lone seeker |

Changes per stage:

- **Stage 1:**
  - Brood becomes a personal attack (a mite pair).
  - A new crane sweep uses her crane arms, which today only show.
- **Stage 2:**
  - Slag becomes a personal attack (a sludge drop).
  - The charge stops being a punish for keeping away and is a regular loop move.
  - Flak is removed.
- **Stage 3:**
  - The dart before every move goes. She repositions once, during the spiral's tell.
  - The iris is no longer its own move: it is the spiral's window.
  - Seekers come in a fixed count.

### 4.2 Signature moves

| Move | Tell (light, always the same) | What it does | Dodge rule (always works) | Window (cap 1,400) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rivet line** (s1) | One wing gun swings round onto you with a ratchet clank; a faint yellow line. Then the next gun, clockwise | Each gun hammers 4 rivets (34 u/tick) down its line; 6 guns, once round (not twice). One gun at a time | **Cross the line** before it fires (lines are drawn at you, never ahead) | **Breeches open:** her wing guns vent grey steam and hang slack, 7 s |
| **Haul** (s1) | Her two magnets hum and glow green (a rising hum), no circle drawn | 100 ticks of pull towards her front (0.55 u/tick at the middle, none at 3,600), not scaled by pilots or magnets; at the end her claws snap shut across her front | **Get out of her front** (side or behind her: the pull is only towards her front, the claws only close there) | **Claws jammed:** they stick shut, sparking, 7 s |
| **Crane sweep** (s1, NEW) | A crane arm's elbow lamp blinks amber twice and the arm draws back | The arm swings across her front half from one side to the other (always starting from the side that drew back), its claw a 260-wide hit along it, reach 800-3,200 | **Get in close** (under 800 from her hull the arm passes over you) | **Overextended:** the arm sticks out and she's turned side-on, 7 s |
| **Intake** (s2) | Her throat glows and her rotors spin up with a rising grind | Her maw's lane (1,000 wide, her front) pulls you towards the rotors; close to the maw you're ground (26 a tick-10 per rotor) | **Leave the lane sideways** | **Choked:** her throat plates blow open coughing scrap, 7 s |
| **Shrapnel rings** (s2) | Her stacks puff black smoke, three puffs | Three rings of 26 chunks (11 u/tick) from her stacks, 1.0 s apart; each ring has two gaps, **always at her left and right flanks** as she faced at the tell | **Get level with her side** (the gap is there every ring) | **Overheated stacks:** they glow dull red, 7 s |
| **Charge** (s2) | Her drives flare white and she backs off a little | Lines up on where you **are** and rams at up to 26 u/tick down her width | **Sidestep** when the drives flare (it doesn't turn once it goes) | **Overshoot:** she slides past and comes about slowly, her drives exposed, 7 s |
| **Scissor** (s3) | Her welder tips glow blue-white with a crackle | Two beams sweep in from wide either side until they cross ahead of her | **Get in close** (inside her welders, nothing crosses you) | **Welders cooling:** they drip white and hang, 6 s |
| **Spiral** (s3) | Her core pulses green twice; she darts once to a new angle | Four arms of chunks spun out of her core, **one way only**, no turn-back, no aimed fans; 200 ticks | **Circle with the spin** at range (the arms pass slowly; you ride the gap) | **Iris open:** the iris opens, breathing green, 7 s (this is the old iris, now harmless) |
| **Seekers** (s3) | Her four spikes chime one after another | 8 seam seekers in all, shared among the pilots she can see (not 8 each), turning slowly (homing 0.045) | **Turn hard across them** (change direction once they commit) | **Spikes spent:** they retract and smoke, 6 s |

Breaking parts still disables moves, as today. Shoot off the magnets and there is no haul; the claws and there is no
snap; the welders and there is no scissor. In a disabled move's slot she rests for 4 s, no window, and goes on. **Without
its move there is no window either**, so breaking a move off makes the fight safer, not shorter.

### 4.4 Arena

Her arena is the **Heart**, the central chamber of the Nest Rock: radius 6,000, walled by rock (section 9.1). Her flight
home is clamped to the chamber's middle with `homeR` 3,500, so her 1,365 radius never touches the walls. The barrier is
across the Throat, the one tunnel from the Gullet.

### 4.5 Dormant

She lies **half-buried in the scrap bowl** at the Heart's middle:

- **Asleep:** only her stage 1 crane arms and two brood racks show above the heap, dark and still. This is scenery: a
  look-only cut-out drawn by the terrain, not an entity.
- **Waking (6 s):** the heap heaves, cranes unfold, her greyseam lights come on one by one with a deep power-up hum,
  and scrap slides off her as she rises. The entity spawns 2 s in, invulnerable, and is engageable at 6 s. Her intro
  card shows then.

## 5. Pyreclast

**Role:** the meta boss.

- **Pressure:** its behaviour pushes you towards one kind of rig: armour penetration for precision part-breaking, heat
  protection, and separate power for engines and guns.
- **Off-meta:** possible, just slower (9.9 against 6.2 min solo).

**Expected ship:** about level 30, with 25-30 u/tick top speed and 8k-20k HP.

### 5.1 Stages

| Stage | HP | Body | Habit | Signature moves | Personal attacks |
|---|--:|---|---|---|---|
| 1, the fortress | 23,400 | carapace wings, wing turrets, scythes, bays, flak | slow, holds off at 2,100 | barrage, broadside, scythe sweep | magma lob, ember drone, flak burst |
| 2, the hunter | 31,200 | body: mortars, dash coils, lance, synapse wires, vents | drives harder, closes in; vents burn close | mortar wall, dash chain, lance sweep | magma lob, synapse mark |
| 3, the heart | 23,400 | heart: core, crystal, rings, vents | small and quick; still for its vent | crystal beams, ring pulse, swarm, then **vent** | spark fan, magma lob |

What goes:

- Stage 1's brood becomes a personal attack.
- Stage 2's synapse lock becomes a personal attack (synapse mark).
- The charge punish is removed.
- Stage 3's spiral (the worst of the bullet hell) is removed. The heart is down to three moves plus its vent, as asked.
- The dart before every stage 3 move goes. It darts once, before the crystal.

### 5.2 Every limb drives an attack

Limbs are armour 5, and each has its own HP. Breaking one removes or weakens its attack.

| Limb | Drives | Broken: |
|---|---|---|
| Wing turrets (6 magma cannons) | barrage, broadside | each one gone: one fewer fan / lane |
| Scythes (2) | scythe sweep | each one gone: that side never sweeps; both gone: no sweep, no window |
| Bays (2) | ember drone (personal) | that side's drones stop; both gone: the personal pool loses it |
| Wing flak (2) | flak burst (personal) | as above |
| Mortars (2) | mortar wall, magma lob in stage 2 | one gone: the wall has no cap; both: no wall |
| Dash coils (2) | dash chain | fewer dashes; none: no chain |
| Lance | lance sweep | no sweep |
| Synapse wires | synapse mark (personal) | gone from the pool |
| Crystal | crystal beams | no beams |
| Heat vents (stage 2 x2, stage 3 x5) | the burn aura; the stage 3 vent window | **cannot be broken while it vents** (the vent is shielded by its own heat); between vents they can be, and each one gone shrinks the aura 20% |

**Limb HP:** a stage's limbs together are about 40% of that stage's deciding HP again. They aren't deciding, so they
are extra, optional work.

**Breaking time:** at pen 4 (x0.55) a level 30 rig breaks a limb (about 1,200-2,500 HP) in 6-10 s of focused fire. At
pen 2.5 (x0.175) it takes 25-40 s, which is why off-meta pilots usually leave limbs alone and dodge everything.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Barrage** (s1) | Its wing turrets glow orange one at a time, clockwise, with a rising roar | Each turret throws a fan of 3 magma balls (16 u/tick) at where you **are**; once round | **Keep moving sideways** (aimed where you are, never ahead) | **Turrets cooling:** red-hot barrels, 7 s (5,200) |
| **Broadside** (s1) | It rolls side-on; that wing's vein glows and its turrets swing in unison | That wing's turrets fire down parallel red lanes together (fast shells only down the drawn lanes) | **Get off that side** (over its nose or tail) | **Recoil:** the broadside shoves it back and its wing plates crack open glowing, 7 s (5,200) |

## 7. Arenas, barriers, recovery and dormancy

### 7.1 The barrier

- **Data:** each lair gets an `arena` block in `data/systems.json` (**NEW**):
  `{boss, centre, radius, barrier: [[x, y], [x, y]], outside: [x, y]}`.
- **Walls:** the arena is the chamber's open rock plus the barrier segment that closes its one tunnel.
- **Solid:** the barrier is solid to ships and to shots, beams, splash, pulls and drones, both ways. It is built into the
  terrain as a `gate` piece, so `collideTerrain`, `rayTerrain` and `insideTerrain` stop everything at it and every
  existing shot path respects it with no new check.
- **No sniping:** with the rock all round, nothing crosses in or out.
- **Look:** the Mother's is a curtain of welded scrap hung in a faint green shimmer. The Pyreclast's is a sheet of
  heat-haze over a lip of cooled slag. Both are lit when you are near, with a prompt.
- **Passing:** you press the interact key within 400 u of it.
  - **Entering is free.**
  - **Leaving** needs you **out of combat for 5 s**: no damage dealt or taken, from any source, for 300 ticks. The prompt
    counts down ("Leaving in 3…") or says why not ("In combat: 2.4 s").
- **Transit:** a pass is a **tick-stamped server decision**: ship, tick T and direction. From T the shared flight step
  carries that ship through on rails for 60 ticks: a fixed path through the gate, controls ignored, like the jump burn.
  Server, prediction and replicas agree, so it is never an own-ship keyframe.
- **Dying inside:** puts you outside. You never respawn inside, and a pilot who logs out inside logs back in at the
  `outside` point.
- **The boss stays in:** its flight home is clamped well inside, and it ignores pulls, tows, the Singularity and
  knockback from players (**NEW** `anchored` on the boss body: outside impulses on it are zeroed in the shared step).
  Its mobs are leashed to the arena and burst at the barrier.

### 7.2 Recovery

Kieran: this replaces any staged-recovery idea.

- **Out of combat:** nobody inside the barrier and nobody has hurt it for 30 s. It **idles 30 s**, then **heals**.
- **Full restore in about 2 minutes from the start of the heal:** both the Mother and the Pyreclast.
  - HP comes back across the stages, latest first (from the stage being fought back to stage 1).
  - Blown-away stages regrow in order. Pieces fade back in, piece by piece, innermost first (the blow-away reversed), at
    most one rebuild per tick for the boss (the rebuild batching from the netcode work).
  - Its windows and phase state reset.
- **Interrupted:** anyone entering, or any damage, stops the heal where it is. The boss fights from there, and the 30 s
  starts again when it is next out of combat.
- **Why 2 minutes and not 10:** 10 minutes would let a pilot die, fetch a new ship and come back to a half-dead boss.

### 7.3 Dormancy

**States:** DORMANT → WAKING → ACTIVE → RECOVERING (the 30 s idle) → HEALING → SLEEPING → DORMANT.

- **Decisions:** each change of state is a server decision stamped with its tick. Clients play the animation from that
  tick, so a pilot arriving mid-wake sees the same frame as everyone else.
- **DORMANT:** no entity exists, so it costs nothing to simulate or send. The lair shows look-only scenery (the half-buried
  heap, the lava ridge) from the terrain file.
- **WAKING:** a pilot crossing the barrier inward wakes it (sections 4.5 and 5.6). The barrier is the one trigger; there
  is no approach radius.
- **SLEEPING:** once healed to full out of combat, it animates back down (6-8 s: sinks into the heap or the lava), and
  the entity is removed at the end.
- **System inactive:** if its system has been asleep at least as long as recovery takes (30 s idle + 120 s heal = 150 s),
  SystemMemory brings it back DORMANT and full, with no animation.
- **Death:** `respawnSeconds` still runs (Mother 600, Pyreclast 900). After it, the lair is simply DORMANT again. No
  "spawn" is seen.
- **Wire:** `bossIntro` is sent at the end of WAKING.

## 8. Recovery for every enemy

This covers packs, AI ships, custom enemies and mobs.

- **Trigger:** an enemy that has neither dealt nor taken damage for **30 s** starts to heal.
- **Rate:** it is **full about 2 minutes after the heal starts**, the same as the bosses.
- **Parts:**
  - Lost parts (grid ships) and lost groups (custom enemies) regrow in the last 30 s of the heal.
  - At most one rebuild per ship per tick.
  - For a custom enemy a regrown group is one bit in its group byte, which already travels as a decision.
- **Sleeping systems:** packs restored from SystemMemory after at least 150 s away come back full. Today they get a
  partial `heal` share.
- **Code:** it lives in a **NEW** `server/src/game/enemies/recovery.ts`, a registry entry the pack tick calls, out of
  the `Enemies.ts` god-file.
