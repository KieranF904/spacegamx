# Rework 2026-11: weapons, parts and energy

**Status:** binding design for the post-refactor rework. Judged from three proposals (feel, identity, systems)
against the owner's brief in `backlog-after-refactor.md`. Where this doc and a proposal differ, this doc wins.
Where it is silent, keep today's behaviour.

**Units and conventions**
- Times at 60 ticks/s; ticks in brackets where it matters.
- **E** is energy on today's scale (core store 240, a Cell Reactor makes 35/s, a Pip shot costs 2 E).
- Every number is what the **Mk I part shows in its hangar tooltip at its own piece** (model numbers are their own;
  grade upgrades and size still scale damage and draw the way `grades.ts` does today). Implementers tune
  `scripts/econ/models.mjs` (`weaponMods`, `fireInterval`, `demand`) and `data/ships/tuning.json` until the tooltip
  shows these numbers; the text snapshots then pin them.
- **Engine, shield, coil and array numbers are per cell of area** (thrust, draw, shield, regen), like generators. A one-cell
  square piece shows them as written; a tri shows half, a two-cell rect double, a block four times, a round about pi/4
  (e.g. the Vernier's 1.2 / 4 shows as 0.6 / 2 on its tri piece, the Torch's 4.0 / 34 as 8.0 / 68 on its rect2). Guns, banks and
  the Deflector are per piece.
- No model ids are added or removed. All 72 models stay. The wire dictionary keeps every id.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **One sentence per part.** Each part has one character and at most one twist, and the hangar card states it in
   one sentence. If a rule needs two sentences, it is cut.
2. **One trigger verb per gun.** The verb is the gun's firing model (section 3). There are five shared models and
   every gun uses exactly one. Twists are options on a shared model, not new models.
3. **Big hitters announce themselves.** Charge guns, landing shells, guided warheads and area weapons show a tell
   that every pilot can see (sight line, landing ring, radius ring, beacon). Small guns need no tell: their volume of
   fire is the tell. NPCs get the same tells.
4. **One visual grammar.** An enemy reads colour and shape before text.
   - Colour = how it hurts: amber/yellow kinetic shells; cyan/blue-white rails and electricity; red/orange lasers;
     pink/magenta shield strippers; grey smoke trail = guided ordnance (Point Defence can kill it); deep blue EMP;
     violet exotic and legendary.
   - Shape = what it does: thin streak fast and precise; fat glowing blob slow and heavy; orb area; continuous line
     held beam; ring on the ground something is landing here.
   - Size and brightness = damage per hit. Muzzle flash, recoil kick (`ShipRenderer.kick`) and impact scale with
     per-shot damage.
   - **The model sets shape and colour; the grade only adds size and brightness** (the generic `weaponStyle()`
     scaling). Cannons lose their per-grade looks (`CANNON_GRADES`) because the five cannons now differ by model.
5. **No heat, no warmth in guns.** Lance and Frostbite heat goes. Guns never read warmth (`GunOrders.slow` goes).
6. **Wiring as before 5896116.** Plus one new layout rule (capacitor banks) that the hangar draws.
7. **Buildable on what exists.** Shared gun logic lives in `common/src/ships/guns.ts`, predicted by the pilot exactly
   as cooldowns are today. Unique per-gun behaviour goes through the registry's weapon roles (`PartFeature.weapon`,
   server `onFire`/`onTick`). Visuals use the existing pools and textures.

### 2.2 One ship pool, with an Output limit

- **The pool** is the core's store plus every Battery and Heavy Bank on a powered network. (The old drive and weapon
  pools are gone; the Drive Cell and Capacitor become bank parts, section 2.3.)
- **Output** is the most the pool can give in one second:
  `Output = spare generation + core 720 + the Gives of each Battery and Heavy Bank that holds charge`.
- **Each tick, in this order:**
  1. **Engines** take what they need first: first from Drive Cell banks they touch, then from Output.
  2. **Guns, beams, mining lasers and part features** (Tow, Strafer extras through `PowerUse.extra`) share what is
     left of Output **equally**, after first drawing from Capacitor banks they touch. A part short of power is in
     **brownout**: it reloads or charges slower, and a beam burns only that share of the ticks (it sputters).
  3. **Spare generation** refills every store, emptiest first: pool batteries and banks alike (a bank only if it
     can refill, section 2.3).
- **How guns pay** (kept from Phase 1, now on one pool): a gun's energy is drawn **while it reloads or charges**
  (`gunLoad`, `feedShare`). A loaded gun always fires when you pull the trigger; low power only slows the next
  reload. This is the one deliberate departure from pre-5896116 (which paid a packet at the shot): it is what lets
  discharge rates matter, which the owner wants kept.
- **One sentence for the hangar:** "Your ship can give out at most its Output each second; engines are served first,
  and when guns want more, they all reload slower."

| Store | Piece | Holds | Gives | Empties in | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core | (ship) | 240 | 720/s | 0.33 s | always there |
| Battery | sq | 200 a cell | 600/s a cell | 0.33 s | the all-rounder |
| Heavy Bank | block2 | 300 a cell (1,200) | 150/s a cell (600/s) | 2 s | deep but slow: endurance, not alpha strikes |

Generators keep today's numbers: Cell Reactor 35/s a cell, Isotope Pile 18, Fusion Plant 48, Overclock Reactor 62.
`energy.boostDraw` stays 2.8. If the library-fight test fails on the Heavy Bank's rate, turn down `boostDraw`, not
the rate.

### 2.3 Capacitor banks: the layout rule

> **Capacitors that touch form a bank. A bank powers the parts touching it before the ship does, as fast as its
> Gives, and refills from spare power.**

Exact rules:
1. **Touch** = sharing a full cell edge. Corners don't count. A tri piece counts its straight edges. This is the
   existing bank adjacency, minus every Surge and dynamo special case.
2. **Two kinds, never mixed.** Capacitors bank with Capacitors and feed **guns, beams, mining lasers and the Flash
   Screen**. Drive Cells bank with Drive Cells and feed **engines and Burst Strafers**. A Capacitor touching an engine
   does not feed it, and the card says so.
3. **A bank's Holds and Gives** are the sums over its members.
4. **Payment.** A fed part draws from its bank(s) first, up to the bank's Gives this tick (fuller bank first when it
   touches two), and takes the rest from Output. Nothing else can drain a bank: boosting the main pool flat leaves a
   gun bank full.
5. **Refill.** A bank refills from spare generation (emptiest store first, as above) when at least one member is on a
   powered network, plus whatever a touching Pulse Dynamo makes. A bank that can do neither gives what it holds,
   then stays empty; the hangar shows it grey with "never refills".
6. **Damage.** A destroyed member's charge is lost; the rest of the bank keeps its share. A repaired member comes
   back empty.

| Part | Piece | Holds | Gives | Feeds | Built for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacitor | sq | 160 | 2,000/s | guns, beams, miners, Flash Screen it touches | punch: a Rail Driver reload, a Longbarrel charge or a Spear shot paid at once |
| Drive Cell | sq | 320 | 1,400/s | engines and Burst Strafers it touches | boosts and dodges (a Strafer burst costs 140: 2 free bursts per cell) |
| Pulse Dynamo | sq | 0 | — | makes 10/s into each bank it touches, split evenly; no wires, powers nothing directly | a self-charging gun pod or strafer pod |

`PowerLayout.feeds[].bank` and `feedShare` already model "bank first, then the circuit". The bank code becomes
"bank first, then the ship".

### 2.4 How the hangar shows it

1. **Wire overlay** (restored): networks in colour, unpowered parts red with a warning line.
2. **Bank overlay** (new, shown whenever the power view is on): each bank gets one outline around its members (amber
   for Capacitors, blue for Drive Cells); a short glowing **strap** runs across every edge to a part it feeds; fed
   parts get a small bolt glyph. Label: `Bank 1 · holds 320 · gives 4,000/s`. Drawn in `drawPower` with the wire
   Graphics.
3. **Placement preview:** while holding a Capacitor, Drive Cell or Pulse Dynamo, the parts it would feed glow cyan.
   If it would feed nothing, a grey tag reads "feeds nothing" (for a Drive Cell: "feeds nothing: it must touch an
   engine or strafer").
4. **POWER panel** (replaces the circuit list):
   ```
   Generation 105/s · steady loads 40/s · spare 65/s
   Store 840 (core 240 + 3 Batteries) · Output 2,585/s
   Flat out: engines 380/s (boosting 1,440/s) · guns 410/s
   ⚠ Boosting while firing: unbanked guns get 62%
   Bank 1 (2 Capacitors) 320 · 4,000/s → Longbarrel, Rail Driver
   ```
   The load test shrinks to the one warning line, from the existing `loadTest` code.
5. **Gun tooltips and cards speak in shots, not energy:** firing-model chip and numbers; "Fed by Bank 1 first";
   "Bank covers 6 reloads"; for charge guns "Charge 2.0 s (2.9 s while boosting without a bank)".
6. **In flight (own ship):** the ENERGY gauge as before; one thin bar per bank (at most 4) under it; each bank
   member's glow tracks its charge (`partDecor` glow alpha). A drained bank visibly dims.

### 2.5 Charge on the wire

`Charge` becomes `{ g, b: number[] }`: the pool's percentage plus one percentage per bank, in bank order (banks
numbered by their lowest part index). The pilot row's count-prefixed charge groups (`CHARGE_GROUPS_AT`,

**Protocol bump** (group meaning changes).

## 3. Firing models

Five shared models plus Point Defence's automatic mode. Each has a chip on its card and HUD.

| Chip | Model | Hangar sentence | Pays with | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚡ | **Cycle** | "Fires on a fixed beat while held; each shot's energy is drawn as it reloads." | power over the reload | `burst {n, gap}`, `salvo`, `spin {from, to, up, down}` |
| ▮ | **Clip** | "Fires n rounds fast, then reloads from energy when empty, or 1 s after you let go." | power over the reload | `regrow {every}` (refills one round at a time instead) |
| ◎ | **Charge** | "Hold to charge; it fires itself at full charge, then cools down. Let go early and nothing fires." | power over the charge | — |
| ◆ | **Ammo** | "Fires rounds from your hold on a fixed beat; costs no energy; empty means weak plain rounds (40% damage)." | one round (or `rounds`) from the hold | `burst`, `salvo` |
| 〰 | **Beam** | "Burns energy every second you hold it." | power per tick | `windup` |
| (auto) | Point Defence | "Fires on its own at incoming ordnance." | power over the reload | — |

### 3.1 Data

One `fire` block per weapon model in `models.mjs`, carried through `WeaponInfo` in rebuild:

```ts
fire: {
  model: 'cycle' | 'clip' | 'charge' | 'ammo' | 'beam' | 'auto';
  interval: number;           // ticks between shots (cycle, clip, ammo)
  clip?: number; reload?: number; reloadE?: number; regrow?: number;  // clip
  charge?: number; cooldown?: number; chargeE?: number;               // charge
  burst?: { n: number; gap: number }; salvo?: number; spin?: { from: number; to: number; up: number; down: number };
  windup?: number;            // beam
  rounds?: number;            // ammo per shot (default 1)
}
```

`ShipState` gains per-part `rounds[]`, `reload[]`, `charge[]` and `spin[]` (numbers, allocated with the parts, as
`cooldown` is). `heat[]` and `overheated[]` are deleted.

### 3.2 Rules and prediction

All five run in shared `stepGuns`, deterministic by tick on the server and on the firing pilot's client (forward-only,
never on a replay), exactly as cooldowns run today. Energy comes from `gunLoad`/`feedShare` (section 2.2).

| Model | Server and pilot step (shared) | Others see | Wire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cycle | Unchanged from today: cooldown counts down at the power share; a loaded gun fires. `spin`: the interval eases from `from` to `to` over `up` ticks of continuous fire, back over `down` ticks after release. | fire events (today's) | none |
| Clip | `rounds` counts down per shot at `interval`. At 0, or after 60 ticks without firing with a partial clip, `reload` runs `reload` ticks at the power share, drawing `reloadE × missing/clip`; then the clip is full. `regrow`: one round back every `regrow` ticks (at the share), each drawing `reloadE / clip`. Rebuild or respawn: full clip. | fire events; a watcher counts a gun's events against its clip size and plays the reload vent on the last round | pilot-row gun-state correction (3.3) |
| Charge | While fire is held and the gun is aligned and off cooldown, `charge` rises by the power share per tick, drawing `chargeE` spread over `charge` ticks. At full: it fires on that tick and `cooldown` starts; still held, a new charge starts after it. Released early: charge drains to 0 over 15 ticks, nothing fires, the energy drawn is spent. | **charge-start and charge-cancel events** (3.3), so the sight line and barrel glow show from the first tick | yes |
| Ammo | A cycle whose cost is `rounds` from the hold (`Ammo.ts` crates); no energy. Empty: dry-fire, plain rounds at 40% damage with effects stripped; the chip goes red. | fire events | none (ammo count already reaches the pilot) |
| Beam | On while held and aligned; draws its E/s per tick; at a share below `BROWNOUT` it burns that fraction of ticks, evenly spread (the sputter). `windup`: damage ramps from 0 over `windup` ticks. No heat, no overheat. | the beam (today's) | heat fields removed |

### 3.3 What the client needs from the wire

- **Gun-state correction** (pilot row, own ship only): count-prefixed `(partIndex, value)` pairs for clip and charge
  guns, at most 8: `rounds` for a clip gun (with the high bit set while reloading), `charge` ticks for a charge gun.
  The client adopts the server's value only when it has disagreed for 30 ticks running (so normal latency never
  snaps a pip). Appended slot, protocol bump.
- **Charge start and cancel:** reuse the shell-less fire event the pulse laser already sends, with sentinel ammo codes
  in `partAmmo` (255 = charge started, 254 = charge cancelled). Semantic bump, no new message.
- **Projectile `endTick`:** appended to the projectile row (`data[9]`): the tick a shell lands or bursts, for shells
  that end at a chosen point (mortars, Cloudmaker, Prism split, Blackout orb). Lets everyone draw landing rings and
  burst points. Bump plus `wire:bump`.

### 3.4 HUD weapon ring

Each gun group gets an arc segment around the crosshair, all predicted locally: clip pips (grey "no power" while a
reload waits on power), a charge arc that fills and flashes at full, an ammo count, a spin arc, and a thin bank bar
under the segment when a bank feeds the group.

All in this rework; land them in as few bumps as the units allow (the game is not deployed between units).

| Change | Why | Kind |
|---|---|---|

| Heat fields (`heat`, `overheated`, the snapshot beam-heat and status-row overheated) removed | No heat | Bump |
| Gun-state correction slot in the pilot row | Clip and charge prediction drift (3.3) | Append + bump |
| Charge start/cancel as shell-less fire events (`partAmmo` 255/254) | Others see Longbarrel and Blackout tells (3.3) | Semantic bump |
| Projectile row `endTick` (`data[9]`) | Landing rings, aim-distance bursts for everyone | Append + bump + `wire:bump` |

| Models' `surge`, `dual` fields removed | Surge cut | Data; wire dictionary keeps every id |
| Not needed | Per-model looks (client derives the model from the fire event's `part`), Hammerhead guidance (server already reads `aimDist` each tick), Chainsaw spin, clip reload visuals | — |

## 7. Visual system (client only, no protocol)

- `MODEL_STYLES[modelId]` overrides `WEAPON_STYLES[kind]` in `client/src/rendering/weapons/weaponStyles.ts`.
  `ShipClient.preSnapshot` knows each fire-event shell's owner and `part`, so the model comes from
  `ship.blueprint.parts[part]`. A client-only `shellId → style` map keeps the look when the server row takes the
  shell over. Shells first seen in a snapshot fall back to the kind style.
- New optional `WeaponStyle` fields, drawn with the existing `ParticlePool`, `fxTextures` (`streak`, `softDot`,
  `disc`, `ring`, `glowBall`, `puff`, `petal`, `bubble`) and the crackle Graphics: `pair`, `casings`, `contrail`,
  `afterimages`, `ringPulse`, `smokeCloud`, `lightning`, `wobble`, `blink`, `arcScale`, `shardHue`.
- Beams: a `BEAM_LOOKS[modelId]` table consulted by `PulseFx` and `MiningBeamFx` (width, colours, braid, motes, cone,
  ramp pool, sputter).
- `ChargeFx`: a small layer (same pattern as `PulseFx`) for charge tells, landing rings, radius rings and the
  Searchlight mark.
- Gun decor states (rail lights, segment ring, honeycomb, reload glow, spinning barrels) via `gunDecor`/`partDecor`
  tint and rotation from the predicted gun state (own ship) or fire-event counting (others).
- Engine plume changes need `ThrusterFx` and the native render port (`scripts/native-render/fx.ts`, `gl_worker.py`).
- Recoil, muzzle flash and impact scale with per-shot damage; heavy shots add a small client-only camera nudge for
  the shooter. Hit confirmation: a crosshair tick sized by damage.
- Capture before/after panels per category in `docs/panels/<date>/<category>/` (stills via native `npm run render`).
