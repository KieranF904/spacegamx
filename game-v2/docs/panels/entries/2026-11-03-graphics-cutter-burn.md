The Cutter's burn on a dummy's hull, 3, 10, 30 and 60 frames into the burn, then half a second and a second after it stops. The beam itself is left out of the frames. Every few frames sparks and a streak splash out of the hull, and glowing chips tear off and trail smoke for about a second. Before, it had only its molten pool and a few sparks round the burn point.
