Every engine model idle, at 0.8 throttle and on boost (native renderer, one stub each): the Reaction Thruster flew the default blue flame and models shared their family's flame, now each has its own: a steady narrow Ion column, crisp Vernier puffs, a roaring orange Afterburner cone that flares long on boost with sparks, the Torch's white-yellow needle, a magenta Plasma stream with travelling pinch rings and swirling motes, the Fusion Torch's narrow blinding core in a blue-violet sheath with a wide haze and lingering trail, the Drift Sail's sheet of light and rolling ripples, and a throbbing pink Reaction Thruster with slugs of reaction mass. The Burst Strafer has no cruise push, so nothing shows here; its sideways gas pops show on a dodge.
