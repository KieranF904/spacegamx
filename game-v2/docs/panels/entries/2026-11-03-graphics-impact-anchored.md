A Bastion and a Spear hit on a moving range dummy (top) and on a rock (bottom), with the camera on the target. Frames are about 0.11, 0.22, 0.33 and 0.44 s after the hit. The dummies steer for themselves and the rock is the one nearest each run's spawn, so before and after are not the same moment. Before, the fireball and sparks stayed where the hit happened while the target flew on, so they slid off it within a tenth of a second. Now the fireball, glow, scorch and light ride the hull or the rock as it moves and turns. The fragments and sprays leave with the target's velocity at the hit point and trail along behind it.
