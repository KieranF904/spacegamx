The same slanting shot into a dummy's hull, frames 3, 10, 30 and 60, by row: Pip, Hammer, Spark, Rail Driver, Longbarrel, Spear, Bastion. Before, a heavy gun's hit was a bigger spark gone in about a quarter of a second, much like a Pip's. Now the size and length of a hit rise with the damage a shot does. The Pip stays a quick flick and the Spark lasts about half a second. The Hammer, Rail Driver, Longbarrel, Spear and Bastion throw a fireball off the hull along the rebound, and 7 to 18 glowing fragments that slow, cool and trail smoke for one to two seconds.
