The same slanting shot into a dummy's hull, frames 3, 10, 30, 60 and 90, by row: Pip, Hammer, Spark, Rail Driver, Longbarrel, Spear, Bastion. Before, every fragment drifted out slowly and hung round the hit for one to two seconds. Now they burst out of the fireball about two and a half times as fast and the typical one is gone in about half its old time. About one in six is an outlier, twice as fast or nearly twice as long-lived, its speed and life drawn separately. The outliers streak and smoke well clear of the frame; at full game zoom they cross the screen and carry a heavy hit to one and a half or two seconds.
