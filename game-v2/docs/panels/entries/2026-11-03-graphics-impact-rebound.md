The same slanting shot into a dummy's hull, frames 3, 7 and 14, by row Rail Driver, Longbarrel, Bastion, Chainsaw, Strobe, Spark. Before, the centred core, glow and shock rings carried the hit and the rebound spray was a side note. Now the rebound leads: bright streaks shoot out along the rebound line (three fanned off the Bastion) and a denser spray follows them. The core and glow are a small brief pinpoint, the shock rings are gone, and the loose sparks keep to the throw.
