The Lance burning into a giant's hull, five fixed moments of one held burst (lighting, burning, gone out, charging, lit again): a violet tube wound with a white zigzag that just stopped and started, now a layered beam (soft haze, violet sheath, lilac body, white core) with crackling edges and energy running along it, a flare and sparks where it lands, a burn that rides the hull and cools, the beam running off down the line when it goes out, and the muzzle charging back up between bursts.
