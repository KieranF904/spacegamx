The Lance's muzzle close up while lit, going out, charging, primed and lighting: a flat violet disc while lit and nothing between bursts, now a white flare with arcs off the prongs, a cooling glow after it goes out, a swelling orb and tightening ring while its buffer fills, a slow ring when it is full, and a flash and shock ring as it lights. The beam now starts at the tip of the barrel.
