Terra Nova from space. Top: the sign-in screen's central body (High). Bottom row: the small sign-in body (72 px radius, shown at 2x), a typical flight view (zoom 0.16) and zoom 1 on the lit limb (Medium). The continents are the same, but the land now has climate: rainforest, temperate green, a dry subtropical belt, boreal forest and tundra, with mountain ranges and snowy peaks. The sea now runs from deep ocean through lighter shelves to turquoise shallows. The polar caps are smaller and come with sea ice. Clouds come in climate bands and wind round cyclones, with wispy edges and shaded flanks. Town lights show on the night side. The coasts no longer catch light as glowing ridges on the night side. Drawn by the planet harness (scripts/planet-proto), the same shader as the game.
