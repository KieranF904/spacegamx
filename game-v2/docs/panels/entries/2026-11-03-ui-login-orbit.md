On the sign-in screen the small sun and the small planet or moon now orbit the big central body together, at the same slow pace (a full turn is an hour), each on its own ellipse through the place it used to sit. After: the sun has crossed the sky (the central body's light follows it) and the small body that left at the far edge has been replaced by another of Sol's, Terra Nova, coming in at the same size. The sun comes back at its corner after crossing. Reduced motion keeps them still, as before.
