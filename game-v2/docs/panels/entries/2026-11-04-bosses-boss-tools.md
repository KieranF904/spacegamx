The shared pieces for the boss rework, in the live dev game (docs/bosses/rework-2026-11.md section 13). All three are new, so there is no before shot. In order:
- The Scrap Mother's arena barrier, the green band across the top, with its card: press X to pass. It is solid both ways for ships and shots, and leaving needs 5 s out of combat. Until the Nest Rock is built, the barrier is a ring 7 km out.
- The weak-point window meter under the boss bar. The cap filled early, so it shows cracked and staggered, with the time left running down underneath. Four dummy pilots at level-30 damage filled it.
- The boss tools panel (Shift+F9, dev only) shows her life, stage, move and window. Its buttons go to a boss, wake it, put it to sleep, heal it, skip the clock, hold it, open a window, jump to a stage, play a move next and set a personal attack on you. They also add dummy pilots and change the clock speed. The damage log is at the bottom.
