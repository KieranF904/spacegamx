The Heart of the Maw redesigned as a bespoke staged boss (design only, not in the game; the live boss is unchanged). Third pass, after "too clean and mechanical" and "the textures are flat": the gold now runs as branching, tapering veins with capillaries, fading from the heart outward so the beat is the brightest thing; the stone is irregular, chipped plates inside the symmetric outline, with an overgrowth on one shoulder; stage 2's chambers are ribbed stone ventricles with deep wet mouths and sinew-and-bone braces; the node is a machined collar with uneven roots grown over it and a smooth core with a feathered light; the knots are crystal clutches, not portholes. Surfaces have real texture now (grain, strata, pits, cracks) through prototype hull tiles for its proposed materials, spliced into the private render bundle only; maw-heart-closeups.png shows crust, a ventricle and the core at play distance. maw-heart-v2-vs-v3.png and maw-heart-v1-vs-v2.png compare the passes. Before and after are at the same zoom (live Heart about 640 units in radius, new one about 1470 to its crust, 2460 to its root tips). Design and the cost of the surfaces for real: docs/bosses/maw-heart-redesign.md.
