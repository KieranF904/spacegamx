The Heart of the Maw redesigned as a bespoke staged boss (design only, not in the game; the live boss is unchanged). Second pass after "a bit chaotic and flat": four-fold about the four axes with two deliberate breaks (the crown ahead, longer roots behind), a few big masses (caps, buttresses, four roots) over stepped plates with lit rims, gold seams as wide channels that light the stone either side and converge on a glowing eye where the core shows through, deep dark throats in the chambers, pale bone in the lattice, and the rubble bed as a crater rim of graded boulders. maw-heart-v1-vs-v2.png compares it with the first pass (v1-maw-heart.png). Before and after are at the same zoom: the live Heart is about 640 units in radius, the new one about 1470 to its crust and 2460 to its root tips. Stand-in materials until its own exist. Fight design in docs/bosses/maw-heart-redesign.md.
