The Heart of the Maw redesigned as a bespoke staged boss (design only, not in the game; the live boss is unchanged). Before and after are drawn at the same zoom: the live Heart is about 640 units across its radius, the new one about 2600 to its root tips and 1250 to its crust, the largest boss. Stills by the native renderer: at rest half buried in its bed, awake, stage 1 half shot (crust calved, a root cut at its knot, the lattice bared), stage 2's four chambers, stage 3's node (2.6x closer), and spent. Stand-in materials until its own exist. Fight design in docs/bosses/maw-heart-redesign.md.
