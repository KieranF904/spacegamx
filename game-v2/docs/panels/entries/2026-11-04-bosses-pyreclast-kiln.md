The Pyreclast's cavern map, before and after the Kiln (docs/bosses/rework-2026-11.md 9.2), from the terrain script's preview. You land at the green dot, and the boss's vault is the red one. The Kiln is a new side chamber off the canyon's west bend, reached through a 3 km tunnel. It has a ring of six floor vents (black glass with glowing cracks) round a slag-crusted cache. The vents erupt in turn, each burning everything within 600 for 3 s. The crust is armour 5: it cracks in about 28 s at pen 4 and 88 s at pen 2.5, and pays ceramic plates, coolant and titanium once per 20 minutes per pilot. Two Clinkers hold the Kiln's floor and two Slag Rays hold its tunnel; pairs of Slag Rays and Clinkers are posted along the canyon. The rest of the cavern is unchanged.
