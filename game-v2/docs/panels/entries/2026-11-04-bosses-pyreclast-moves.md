The reworked Pyreclast in the live dev game (docs/bosses/rework-2026-11.md 5.7). There is no before shot: the old moves are gone, not restyled. Top row, stage one: the broadside's lanes straight out of one wing (get over its nose or tail); the scythe's tell, its root glowing and a faint line where the sweep starts; the scythe's blade, drawn only over its reach (get in close). Bottom row, the heart: a ring pulse with its gaps at its nose and tail; its vents open, the big window, with the meter under the bar; a magma lob on a pilot, the ground left burning.
