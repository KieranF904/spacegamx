Eight custom-geometry enemies (design only, none in the game), intact and then damaged. Top row: Scrap Crawler, Rivet Sower (Scrapborn), Slag Ray, Clinker (the Pyreclast's cavern). Bottom row: Censer (Cinder Cult), Maw Lurker, Hookboat (pirates), Rime Bell (Frost Brood). Built from bespoke pieces, so their pieces are drawn from their own shapes and changes to the player ship rules leave them alone. 6-14 parts each, at most 8 destroyable groups, and losing a group changes what the enemy does. Each fits its own frame. Stills from a private native renderer reading designs from docs/enemies/designs. Roster, roles and build notes in docs/enemies/custom-roster.md.
