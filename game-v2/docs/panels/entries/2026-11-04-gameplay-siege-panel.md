Siege day panel up close (Selene day 1, zoom 1): turrets now go on the pad you pick. Each pad is named by its side of the bastion, an empty pad's Build menu lists the four turret kinds and their costs (open here on pad 4), and a built turret shows its reach and an orders button (nearest, closest to the bastion, toughest, fastest).
