The Heron's Cutter with fire held for about 4 s, frames left to right (about 0.2-0.3 s apart), the weapon ring round the cursor. Before, the beam burned its whole buffer as one steady 2 s line, then rested and recharged, and the ring showed one plain arc. Now the buffer is three pieces: each press spends a whole one and the beam burns for exactly a third of the old burn time, swelling to a bright, wide peak at the centre of each piece (where its damage spikes) and thinning again; held, the three chain back to back, then a 0.5 s cooldown (the thin grey arc inside the ring) before it charges. The ring shows three segments: whole pieces lit, the one burning white and shrinking, a part-filled piece as a dim amber sliver (it can't fire, and is thrown away when a whole one is spent).
