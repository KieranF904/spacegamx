Sixteen guns fired upward at once in empty space at the dev range, frozen one frame later (top row: Rattler, Stinger, Pip, Twinlink, Needle, Hammer, Spear, Longbarrel; bottom row: Bastion, Dart, Hammerhead, Eel, Leviathan, Phase, Singularity, Hush). A muzzle flash used to grow in step with the shot's size and damage with no limit, so a Leviathan launch threw a flash 40 times a Rattler's and lit a quarter of the screen. Now flashes follow the shot through the usual range, the light streams have a floor so they still show beside their tracers, and the heaviest launches grow more slowly. The Leviathan is still the biggest, at about one and a half times a Bastion (which comes down a little too). The mid-sized guns are unchanged.
