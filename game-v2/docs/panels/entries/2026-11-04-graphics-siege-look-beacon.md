A front's ground beacon (new): for the last 20 s before a front lands, a red ring draws in on the landing point with dashes turning round it, a pillar of red light and a red lamp on the ground; the night lane leads from it to the bastion. When it lands a shock ring goes out and the night bar says where it came down.
