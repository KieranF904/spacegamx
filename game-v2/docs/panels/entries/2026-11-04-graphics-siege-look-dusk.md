Dusk on Selene: the light goes warm before it goes cold (each world has its own dusk and night tint, on the ground and on the ships' lighting), and tonight's title is a red band across the screen: the siege's name, the night, what's new tonight; roles and the boss show as chips and a badge on nights that have them.
