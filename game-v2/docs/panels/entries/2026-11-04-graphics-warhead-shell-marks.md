Eight warheads striking at once, with their detonations as the server sends them, frozen four frames later (top: Swarm, Dart, Hammerhead, Eel; bottom: Leviathan, Lobber, Quake, Cinder Bloom). Where the shell struck, each one used to draw an old flat flash on top of its explosion: a filled disc sized by the shot with no limit, plus a pile of loose sparks. A Leviathan's disc reached three times past its own blast. Now the shell leaves a small white-hot point, a short glow in its own colour and a few embers, and the explosion is the blast as it always was. In a heavy test fight this cut the render cost of the effects by about two thirds.
