Pelagia, the new water world. Left: an ordinary ocean world's sea level with the same colours (a continent, shores and rainforest, as Terra Nova draws). Right: the water look: the sea stands high so only a few island chains show, ringed by turquoise shallows over deep navy water, more cloud, a broad sun glint with a sheen round it, and the sky mirrored toward the limb. Top: the whole planet; bottom: its lit limb up close. Drawn by the planet harness (scripts/planet-proto, set `water`), the same shader as the game, High level. Terra Nova and Halcyon look the same as before (the new `sea` look field defaults to the old values).
