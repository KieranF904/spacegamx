The ship panel and the ENERGY gauge on a Lancer Frigate, three moments: boosting and firing for 3 s with the store run dry (top), 0.6 s after letting go (middle), and 5 s later (bottom). Before, the gauge just sat empty and nothing said that the engines and guns were getting less than they asked; the panel showed no energy at all. Now the gauge turns red and reads LOW POWER while anything is short, and a line under the schematic gives the charge, the measured rate (−456/s, +429/s), what is short or how long until the stores are empty or full, and the spare power once full. A brownout is held for 0.5 s so the every-other-tick shortage of a boost on an empty store reads steadily. The schematic is a little shorter to make room.
