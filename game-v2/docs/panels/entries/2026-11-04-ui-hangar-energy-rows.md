The hangar's ENERGY rows for the Lancer Frigate. Before: Store and Output (repeated from the POWER section just above), then "brownout at 1.8 s · down to 36%". After: Store and Output stay in POWER only, the rows say "full power 2.7 s, then 38%", and a new Refills row says how long the spare generation takes to fill every store from empty (5.0 s). The boost time is longer because the Lancer's two Heavy Banks now hold 1,800 each (was 1,200): see docs/audits/energy-audit-2026-11-04.md.
