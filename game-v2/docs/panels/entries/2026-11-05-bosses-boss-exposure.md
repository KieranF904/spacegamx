A boss's window now shows where to shoot (docs/bosses/rework-2026-11.md section 15). Before, only the meter under the boss bar showed it. After, the pieces laid open blaze: a white-hot pulsing light, a crosshair glint, a beacon ring and sparks. They are the Scrap Mother's magnets and guns in stage 1 and her core in stage 3. For the Pyreclast they are its wing veins and turrets in stage 1, and its core and crystal in stage 3. Filling the cap early gives a crack: a flash, shards and a shock ring, then a dim stutter while the boss is staggered. Clients draw all of it from the window message they already get, with nothing more sent. The before shots are the same moment with the new effect switched off.
