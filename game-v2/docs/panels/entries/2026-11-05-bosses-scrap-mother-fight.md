The Scrap Mother's reworked fight, live in her arena (docs/bosses/rework-2026-11.md section 4). Before, a barrage drew thick lanes with filler fire between moves. After, the barrage draws one gun's faint line at a time, with nothing between moves. The rest, in order:
- Stage 1: the crane's tell (the elbow lamp blinks amber), its swing (the band it hits is lit; under 600 from her hull it passes over you), its window (the weak-point meter under her bar), and the haul's tell (her magnets glow green).
- Stage 2: the shrapnel's tell (her stacks puff smoke), its rings (always open at her flanks) and the intake's faint lane.
- Stage 3: the spiral (four arms, one way only) and its window (her iris opens).

Two small mites hunting one pilot are a personal attack.
