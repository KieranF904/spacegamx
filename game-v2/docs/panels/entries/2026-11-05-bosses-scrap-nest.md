The Scrap Mother's nest, live. Before, it was a loose field of 1,100 rocks around an open hollow. Now it is the Nest Rock, one hollowed asteroid (`scripts/terrain/scrap_nest.mjs`). In order:
- Before and after at the Throat. The after shows the tunnel into her arena, with her barrier across it: a curtain of welded scrap over the green shimmer.
- Before and after at the way in. The after shows the Mouth on the rock's south face, with plates welded around it.
- The Sorting Floor, with its conveyor belts, and then with a Darter pair out of its heap.
- The Smelter, with its slag pool and three Greyseam Lodes, the den's rich rocks.
- A ring tunnel, lined with amber work lamps.

Every tunnel is at least 3,000 wide. The chambers have floors, greyseam veins and rust in the walls, and cables slung across them.
