Helios Drone (Sol), a new custom design now in every helios_drone spawn, including the training drone. The top image is at play zoom (1) and not resized. From the left: the starter ship, the grid drone it replaces intact and then damaged, and the new drone intact and then damaged (torch arm and right panel gone). It is meant to look like Directorate maintenance kit: a titanium service chassis with gold trim, a sensor dome lit on one half and dead on the other, a jointed welding arm, two folding solar panels and one small drive. Its radius is 71, just under the starter's. It has 11 parts in 6 groups. Its one move is the torch: it noses in slowly and welds whatever comes within reach. scripts/bespoke/enemies/helios_drone.mjs.
