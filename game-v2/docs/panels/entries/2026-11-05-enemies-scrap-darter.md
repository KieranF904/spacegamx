The Scrap Darter (a working name), a new Scrapborn flanker for the Scrap Mother's den. The Crawler and the Sower are both slow, so the den needed something fast. It darts onto your flank, draws a line at you and fires three rivets down it, then peels away. Its pieces each change it:
- Without the gun, it rams.
- Without a fin, it overshoots its turns.
- Shooting its fuel pod bursts it on whatever is beside it.

The stills show it intact, damaged (a fin and the pod gone), and at play zoom. Native renders.
