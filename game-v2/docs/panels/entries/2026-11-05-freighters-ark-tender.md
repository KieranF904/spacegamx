The Ark Tender, the Hauler's leestone-plated sister (the Ark's traffic and the Grey Hour wreck). It was the slowest ship in the game, and boosting made it slower still. It gets the Hauler's fixes (Drive Cells behind the engines, reactors down the flanks, jump coils, a shield and four turrets), and its two side engines are now Fusion Torches, so the heavy stone hull keeps up with the Hauler.
