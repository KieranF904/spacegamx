The Ceres Hauler, the freighter that flies Sol's trade loops and the escort jobs. Holding boost used to make it slower than cruising: its engines ran out of power in under a second. Now a row of Drive Cells sits behind the engines and six Overclock Reactors run down the flanks, so boost is a real gain. It also has two jump coils (its range was the shortest in the game), a Bulwark Projector shield, and four autocannon turrets. Same hull, same 530 hold. Native renders.
