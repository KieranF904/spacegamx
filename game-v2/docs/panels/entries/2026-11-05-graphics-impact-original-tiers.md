Shell hits on a giant's plating at the dev weapons range, original vs now: left to right Pip, Hammer, Twinlink, Longbarrel, Bastion, the same shot number of each burst in both. Before is the client from before any impact work (553b1ed, the commit before e0f4baf); after is today's (refactor/split 024c944). Every gun used to land as one or two flat glowing discs (a Pip's as big as a Hammer's, the Bastion's an orange disc over the whole screen); now a small gun gives a small spark, a mid gun a core and a spray, a heavy gun the full layered hit, and each hit scorches the plate it struck. Live game, Vite dev client and dev server, the Ironmonger dummy, zoom 1.6.
