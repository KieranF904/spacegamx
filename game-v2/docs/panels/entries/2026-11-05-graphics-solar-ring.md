The Helios Array, the solar ring round Sol, same cost or less (much less a frame). Left: as it was; right: a megastructure of collector arrays. Each of its 120 segments has six modules of photovoltaic cells in the shared solar-cell look (solarCells.ts, as on the stations and the Helios Drone), each in a thin bevelled frame with its own slight tilt, so some catch more sheen than others, and a slow sweep of sheen runs round the ring. A spoke carrying a gold power conduit runs up the middle, a dark backing frame sits between the rows, a sunlit inner rim ties the arrays together, and a Warren truss forms the main ring, with a red or green beacon blinking on the outer chord. Every tenth segment is a service hub: an octagon with plating seams, gold trim, corner lamps, a gold docking ring and a slow amber beacon, a mast inward and two ribbed radiators outward, away from the sun and warm at the root. The Halden arc (segments 41-49) has slate slabs with notched corners and a greyseam chevron. Everything that faces the sun is lit and the far side falls into shade. Because the sun is the ring's centre, that light is worked out once when the ring is built, and the shader takes the system's ambient and sun colour from Lighting. It's one mesh and one small shader (rendering/solarRing.ts, geometry in solarRingGeometry.ts), and the same on every quality tier. It now turns by the server clock, so the system map (ui/mapStructures.ts) draws it as it is: a navy band of arrays with its sunlit edge, the truss, gold hubs where they really are, and the Halden arc in slate. Visual only: nothing about it collides, and the jump charging ring (the solar charge zone) is unchanged. Shots: live game (scripts/boss-shots/shoot.mjs), ring turn pinned the same for before and after; the map's "before" is from the same session before the change.
