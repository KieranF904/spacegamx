New belt rock, Taenite: small, dense nickel-iron, hardness 1, about 5 in 109 rocks of Sol's belt. Left: Sol as it was (the Taenite spots are Chondrite there, same place and size); right: the new rock, a pale brassy metal with a pitted, glinting surface, easy to tell from a brown Chondrite at a glance. Drawn by the rock harness (scripts/rock-proto, `ROCK_ONLY=taenite-pair,taenite-belt node scripts/rock-proto/run.mjs stills <dir>`, then compose.py): the game's own drawAsteroid and rock shaders. The first panel is a Chondrite beside a Taenite up close; the second a patch of Sol's belt.
