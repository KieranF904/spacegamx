The hangar's part palette redone as a component browser (Kieran: "incredibly unintuitive"). It shares the component guide's logic (categories, level gates, costs, default shapes are one set of modules now) but is laid out for building, not reading. Six category tabs (Structure, Weapons, Engines, Power, Shields, Utility) with how many are open to you. The list shows what you can place first (spares on hand, then what you can fabricate, its cost red where you're short), then LOCKED: dimmed, each saying "Reach level N", foldable. Picking something that comes in several shapes fills the SHAPE strip under IN HAND: each shape's outline turned as R and F turn it, its footprint (2×1...), a default ticked (the shape you last used it in, else a square, else its first). A row's chevron opens its build facts, read from the data and rules: footprint, hull, armour, mass, damage and fire, thrust, power and ports, mounting rules, facing, exhaust, limits, and its cost against what you have. Tab from the grid focuses the tabs; arrows move inside tabs, shapes and the list. Placement, rotation, overlays, validation and saving are unchanged. Before had an All filter and YOUR PARTS / STRUCTURE / FABRICATE HERE / LOCKED sections, so "all" is before only and "facts" after only. Proto pilot at level 1, free build off, armour in a slope for "shapes", the Hammer (level 6) for "locked".
