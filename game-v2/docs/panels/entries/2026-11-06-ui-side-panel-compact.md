The side panel made compact (Kieran: "too big", items "about half as tall"). Component rows are 30 px instead of 54: the picture is a quarter of its drawn size, the materials sit on the name's line, the gate goes on the line under it, the level is a plain number, and the "Level 1 · 39 components" line moved into the category row's tooltip. That makes 3 rows at 1280x800 where 1 fit before, and 5 at 1920x1080. Friend and party rows are 18 px instead of about 27, with smaller buttons, headers and inputs. The Pilot readout is three columns of tighter cells. The tab bar is slimmer and the default body is 200 px (the least is 80). Before is the first version of the panel (376fe05), with the same tab and window size.
