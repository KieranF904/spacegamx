A tabbed side panel on the left of the HUD, between the pilot readout and the cargo panel (client/src/ui/hub/: HubPanel, a tab registry, one module per tab). Social: the friends list (online with Away/Busy, offline), your status as a button with a menu, "+ Add friend" and requests to accept or decline at the top; click a name to message them, right-click for Message, Invite to party, Remove. Party: your party with each member's hull and options, invite by name, pilots nearby (Invite, Trade, Fly to) and the perks folded away. Pilot: the Esc menu's old Pilot & ship page, live, two columns. Components: the component guide, pictures at half size with tight rows and one sideways-scrolling row of categories. P opens Social and C Components (again: fold); the Social window and the guide's window are gone, and the Esc menu loses Pilot & ship and Component guide. It folds to its tab bar, its top edge drags to resize, and it remembers the fold, the height and each pilot's tab. It never covers the cargo panel: at 1080 tall it sits under the readout; at 1280x800 it shows the tab bar until you open a tab, then lifts over the pilot readout (drawn opaque); in a window too small for it (960x600) it hides until P or C calls it up over the HUD, Esc puts it away. Tab buttons take keyboard focus with a visible ring (arrow keys move along). Shots in proto mode with made-up friends; before is 3a71b49 (the 960x600 and 800x500 befores show the cargo panel already overlapping the readout, not changed here). Made more compact afterwards: see "side panel compact".
