Friends, status and private messages. The P window is now Social, with Party and Friends tabs: friends online (Away and Busy shown with their colour) and offline, your status (Online, Away, Busy, Appear offline), add by name, and requests to accept or decline; click a name to message them, right-click for Message, Invite to party and Remove friend. The chat's tabs are now toggles (All, Public, Private, Game) with a Friends button showing how many are on. Private messages mix into the feed in cyan ("From Wren", "To Sora"), never as bubbles; Tab replies to whoever sent the last one, with "Reply to Wren:" before the input (Backspace at the start or Esc drops it). Shots from a scratch dev lobby and server with made-up pilots (three of them plain WebSocket clients), so there's no before; the last one has only Private on.
