Fast guns, flak fragments, the Cinder Bloom, the Swarm and the Searchlight. The Rattler, Chainsaw and Stinger fire much faster shots (reach unchanged) that draw a long tapered tracer, with a spiked star of a muzzle flash and a wisp of haze; the Twinlink has the same look at its old speed. Flak fragments now fly to their own lengths, some stopping well short of the damage radius. The Cinder Bloom throws a gout of thin licks of fire with fireballs rolling out and embers that cool slowly, its shell drips embers as it flies, and its burst opens in three waves of fire lobes with thrown cinders, a drifting ember cloud and soot. The Swarm's missiles are larger with a brighter glow and a hot contrail. The Searchlight is a wider gold beam inside a shaft of light with dust glittering in it and a spot of light on what it lights. The Searchlight's 'before' strip was captured without the lasers' own colours loaded, so it shows red where the game showed a thin gold line. Each image is four moments of one shot from the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the combined image has before on top and after below.
