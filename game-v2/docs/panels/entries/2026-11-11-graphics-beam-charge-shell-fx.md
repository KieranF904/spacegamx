Second firing effects pass: beams, charge tells, shell halos and flak fragment trails. A held beam's body is five strokes falling off outward so its edge is soft, and its muzzle has an emitter: a soft glow round a hot core, a short flickering flare across the muzzle and a ring thrown off as the beam lights (the repair beam's is calmer); hit glows and the pulse and mining muzzle glows are soft-edged instead of flat discs. The hangar preview now draws the game's own beam body and emitter. A charge gun with no look of its own shows light gathering at the muzzle (a ring closing, motes falling in, a brightening core) in place of a plain dot; the Longbarrel sight and the Blackout tell are unchanged. A shell's halo is one soft dot under the tracer in place of two flat discs over it. Flak fragments leave a soft trail from the burst to where they end, fading over a third of a second after the fragment is spent; they still end exactly at the damage radius. Each image is four moments from the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the combined image has before on top and after below.
