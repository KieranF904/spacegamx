Weapon firing effects, redone by kind of gun. A powder gun throws a ragged, flickering flame with balls of burning gas rolling out of the bore, a smoke plume and white-hot fragments that cool as they fly; a heavy gun (Hammer, Bastion, Quake, Magma) also vents a jet of fire, smoke and sparks to each side and its barrel smokes afterwards; an autocannon has a small star with a wisp of haze; a rail has a needle of light, crackling arcs, ion sparks and vapour along the path; a launcher blows its ignition gas back in a smoke ring with embers; an energy gun has a flare, rays, drifting motes, a ring and an afterglow. Flashes last about a third longer and most are as big as or bigger than before. The flak burst has no ring outlines: a white pop, a lumpy fireball of seven lobes, sixteen fragments thrown at uneven angles and speeds that slow to a stop at the damage radius dropping smoke behind them, a spray of small shrapnel and a lingering dark puff. Each image is four moments of one shot from the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the combined image has before on top and after below.
