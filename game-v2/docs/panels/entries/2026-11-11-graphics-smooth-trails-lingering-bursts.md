Smooth shell trails, bursts that hang longer, a harder-thrown Mauler smoke bank and flak shells drawn from the gun. A shell's contrail was drawn as one round-ended length per frame, and the ends overlapped and doubled up into a row of bright dots; the lengths are now square-ended and join evenly. The glow, fire, smoke and scorch of every weapon hit and detonation hang 1.6 times as long (sparks and fragments unchanged). The Mauler throws more muzzle smoke, much faster, and its swept smoke is carried further after the shell. Your own flak shells are now drawn from the muzzle to the burst point instead of appearing near the burst (live gun range shot, after only). The Mauler close-up 'before' was captured at half the zoom of the 'after'. Strips are four moments of one shot from the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the combined images have before on top and after below.
