Longer tracers, a bigger Longbarrel, a new cannon and smoke behind flak shells. The Rattler, Chainsaw, Stinger and Twinlink draw tracers about five times longer and half again as bright. The Longbarrel's shell is half again as large, with a wider glow and a longer wake, and its flash is a heavy side-vented blast with a jet of sparks and smoke thrown down the line of fire. The Mauler is a new mid-level cannon: a large bright shell with a long wake and swept smoke, a bright flash with a bank of smoke, and a shell that bursts where it lands in uneven lobes of fire, sparks and smoke. Flak canisters now leave puffs of smoke swept along their path before they burst. Each image is four moments of one shot from the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the combined images have before on top and after below.
