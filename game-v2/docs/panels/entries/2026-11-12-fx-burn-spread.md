The Burn modifier's fire now spreads across a hull and shows it. Before: a burning part had a few small flames and nothing moved. After: each burning part has flames, a steady glow, embers and smoke; fire jumps to joined parts, cooler each time, and dies down; a burned-out part is left charred. First pair: a Pip with Burn and Pierce down the line of three, where each hull now lights a few layers deep. Second pair: Burn alone fired down the same line, a raider well alight. Third pair and the later frame: Burn alone on the Mammoth, the fire moving to a neighbouring part after the gun stops. Shots with shoot.mjs in the gun range on the dev server.
