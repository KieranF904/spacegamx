The Mauler looked like a rocket launcher: its shell flew on a long tapering flame with a burning orange wake and a line of smoke swept after it, its flash was a long tongue with smoke thrown down the line of fire, its barrel was the Pip's thin one on a four-cell block, and its burst was a rolling fireball. It now reads as a large cannon. The gun has one great open bore in a reinforcing jacket with a heavy collar at the muzzle. The shell is a stubby glowing slug with a driving band, a bow of hot air across its nose and a glint as it spins. It flies on long straight trails shaped as a heavy round's, not a motor's: a thin white-hot tracer of one width about 350 units long that fades along its length, a faint line of cut air either side of it, a thin pale line left hanging down its path for two thirds of a second, a small ripple thrown off every few ticks, and flecks of hot metal shed and cooling. A first pass (the mid images) had a bare slug on a short tracer, which was too little. The muzzle blast is short and fat, with the brake's side jets, two pressure rings, a bank of smoke that hangs at the bore and a crown of sparks. A hit throws a shock ring and heavy fragments off the hull, and the burst is a hard flash, a shock ring out to the blast's edge, a brief fire and the casing thrown out as fragments. Damage, speed, range, timing and the blast radius are unchanged. The strips are four moments of one shot on the hangar gun preview stage (client/src/dev/fxStage.ts, scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs); the gun images are the native renderer's; the live images are the gun range on the dev server; the last image is the weapon modifier layers on the new shell (burn and velocity, explosive and heavy, pierce and chain).
