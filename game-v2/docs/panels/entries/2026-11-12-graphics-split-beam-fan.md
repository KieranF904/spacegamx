A Split modifier on a held beam now fires a real three-beam fan. Before, the beam stayed a single line and only its damage changed. Each beam of the fan burns its own target for its share of the damage, and the side beams draw a little thinner than the middle one. Shown on the Searchlight and the Cutter at full Split, four frames of a held burn.
