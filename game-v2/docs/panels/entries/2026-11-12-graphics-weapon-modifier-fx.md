Weapon modifiers each add their own layer to any gun's shot, at the muzzle, in flight and at the hit, on top of the gun's own look. Velocity: a tight forward cone and speed streaks, a longer tracer with a wake, a narrow spray punched through the far side. Rapid: stuttering secondary flashes and extra casings, peppering hit sparks. Explosive: an orange-hot core dribbling embers, a lumpy fireball with burning fragments and smoke. Split: a three-way coloured fork, slivers on the shell, triple splinter bursts. Pierce: a needle flash, a bright core line, an entry puff and an exit jet. Wave: crescent fronts ahead of the shot and a wash across what it hits. Chain: crackling arcs at the muzzle and shell, forked lightning at the hit. Heavy: a slow thump with heavy smoke and dust, a dark shell, a deep hit with chunky debris. Burn: flame at the muzzle, a fire trail, flames clinging to the target. Hyper: a line flash down the whole path with an afterimage and a pinpoint hit. Beams get the same ten along the beam and at its end. Each sheet is one gun's first shot, frames left to right, one effect per row: "a" is velocity, rapid, explosive, split, pierce; "b" is wave, chain, heavy, burn, hyper. Before is the gun with no modifiers, after is every effect at full strength, and the third image is the same effects at strength 0.3. The stacked sheets show four effects at once (velocity, explosive, chain, burn; then rapid, split, heavy, hyper) under the plain gun. Captured on the fx stage with `node scripts/fx-shots/strips.mjs <outDir> mods <zoom> <frames> <effects> <model>`. The two live shots are the Mod Showcase dev ship firing on the dev server: its Pip with velocity, rapid, explosive and split together, beside a chain Rattler, a pierce Hammer and a heavy Stinger.
