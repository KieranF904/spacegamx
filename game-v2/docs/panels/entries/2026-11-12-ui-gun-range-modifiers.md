The gun range's weapon picker has a Modifiers row: every effect the pilot may fit as a chip, up to four on at once, each at full strength on every shot. Before and after: the picker without and with the row (nothing on). The third image is a Pip firing with Split, Explosive and Burn on, its mounts, blocks and hopper built on behind the gun; ammo-gun is a gun that fires on ammunition alone, which takes no modifiers on any ship: the chips are greyed and say why. Shots with shoot.mjs on the dev server.
