Weapon modifier parts have their own art and the hangar says what they do. Before and after: a sample of the parts as plain panels, then with their hardware (mounts with a socket on each open edge, each modifier block its own shape and colour, hoppers showing their material). The third image is all fourteen mount, block and hopper parts; line-tiers is each power line part at tier I, II and III; showcase is the dev ship mod-showcase-1. The hangar shots (after only) show: the marks on the design (a square on each socket in its block's colour, a diamond on a fused mount, a pip for each effect on a gun); a gun's tooltip listing its effects in order with strength and what each does; a fused mount (sockets filled, the effect and what fused, treated E/s, upkeep, guns reached); a hopper (what it feeds, refilled when docked); a line part (effects and treated E/s carried); the same ship with its hopper removed and a tier I Conduit, with the dropped effects named on the gun, the line part and the MODIFIERS panel; and the "How modifiers work" guide open beside the Modifiers part list. Parts drawn with `npm run render -- model:<id>` and `ship:dev/mod-showcase-1`; hangar shots with shoot.mjs on the dev server (free build).
