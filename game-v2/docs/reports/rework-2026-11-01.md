# Rework report, 2026-11-01

Branch `refactor/split`, 104 commits since the rework started. Design: `docs/design/rework-2026-11.md` (binding). Brief: `docs/design/backlog-after-refactor.md`.

## What changed

**Energy.** One ship pool (the core plus every Battery and Heavy Bank on a powered network) with an Output limit: spare generation plus the pool's rate while it holds charge. Touching Capacitors form a bank that feeds the guns, beams, miners and Flash Screens they touch first; touching Drive Cells form a bank that feeds engines and strafers first. The two never mix. Pulse Dynamos put 10/s into each bank they touch. Wires are back (W tool, Auto-wire). Surge, circuits and beam heat are gone.

**Firing models.** Every gun now has one of six models (cycle, clip, charge, ammo, beam, auto) that the hangar, tooltips, HUD ring and prediction all share. Empty ammo guns fire weak plain rounds (40% damage), as before.

**Shot behaviours.** Five new roles: end at aim distance, walk (Quake), guided (Hammerhead), marked and slowed statuses, and a projectile end tick on the wire.

## Found and fixed by the audit

- Chainsaw cost 2.8 E a shot, now 1.4. Repair Beam drew 56 E/s, now 28.
- Singularity flew a fixed 228 ticks instead of to the cursor; new `hold` option.
- Field guide said empty ammo guns fall silent; text and design doc now say weak plain rounds.
- Dead beam-heat code and the `beamHeat` hazard removed.
- Hangar Energy block still used the old battery wording; rewritten from the power layout.
- Dodge tooltip still said "circuit" and "capacitor bank"; now Drive Cell bank.
- Capacitor text left out the Flash Screen and "does not feed engines"; fixed in four places.
- Every weapon card now shows its firing-model chip and a one-sentence model line in the tooltip.
- Frostbite card names its slow.
- A reload adopted from the server now resumes at the server's progress (wire change, protocol 20).
- Per-frame allocations in the gun ring and gun sync removed.
- Singularity and Leviathan heads are dark, not white.
- Contrails now linger in the world (Longbarrel 0.8 s, Needle 0.4 s, Spear 1 s).
- Reactors have distinct colours; Capacitor is amber. Unread `BeamLook.plus` removed.
- Spaced Plating is grade 2 (armour 3, below Reactive's 4); design text amended to say the bonus is over the grade.

## Left, and why

- Native stills show housings only. PartFx glows, projectiles, beams and live effects are not drawn by the native renderer, so no after-panel exists for them; entries are marked skipped, and these need a browser run.
- No visual change in this rework has been looked at on screen (no browser was run overnight).
- Twinlink alternating barrels and pair tracers, Bastion vent puffs, the Demolition blink, and client prediction of the stuck charge are not built or not checked.
- Spark is not true hitscan (it would need a new shot path in parity).
- Tesla zap cadence follows the server tick, so the first zap can be up to 11 ticks after pressing fire.
- Pre-existing per-tick allocations in `stepGuns`, `gunLoad` and the `powerTick` result remain.
- The hangar energy durations treat engines and guns as one group each (approximation).
- Panel PNGs are gitignored by project rule, so the before/after pictures exist only on this machine.

## Needs your judgement

- Tuning: Output and bank rates, Chainsaw spin curve, Cutter ramp, Tesla chain, Hive salvo, Singularity hold time, shield refill rates, Spaced Plating (armour 3) vs Reactive (4).
- Tooltip numbers at own piece: the design was reworded to match the data rather than the other way round; confirm that is what you want.
- Looks: every new weapon, engine, power and shield look, and the weapon ring. Check them in a live game.
- Reactor colours (Cell cyan, Isotope green, Fusion pale gold, Overclock red) are my choice.

## How to try it

Run the dev build. F9 opens the ship swap panel, which has the test ships (for example `test-surge-striker`, `dev-test-support`). Use a dev server with free build (proto mode, or an admin with the free-build toggle) to place any part without a licence. Hold fire to see the ring. In the hangar press W for wires or use Auto-wire, then hold a Capacitor or Drive Cell to see which parts it would feed.
