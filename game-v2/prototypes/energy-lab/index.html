Rules this lab follows
  • Power only moves between parts that touch (a shared edge; corners don't count). There is no ship-wide pool.
  • Generators make power every second and push it into the parts touching them. What nothing takes is wasted.
  • Stores and Conduits pass power on. Each takes in no more than its Takes a second and gives out no more than its Gives, so the narrowest part in a chain sets the rate. Touching stores even out towards the same fill.
  • A Diode is a one-way Conduit. Its arrow is its out side: power leaves only that way, into the part across it (a store, a Conduit or a part that uses power). Every other side only feeds it: generators may push into it, stores and Conduits may pass power into it, and nothing comes back. A part touching a Diode's other sides gets nothing from it. Use one to keep guns from draining the engines' stores, or to let power run one way down a spine. When placing one, R turns the arrow; select a placed one and press R to turn it.
  • Weapons, engines, shields and tools are dead ends: they draw only from the generators, stores and conduits touching them, and pass nothing on. Generators are used first, then the fullest store.
  • Order each tick: thrusters, then shields and tools, then weapons.
  • Thrusters burn from a buffer that refills no faster than their Intake. While it lasts they give full thrust; once it's empty, thrust drops to Intake ÷ Burn. Main engines run on Thrust (and Boost), Side Jets while Strafe is on, Burst Strafers for a moment on each Dodge.
  • Weapons fire only when their buffer holds a full shot and their cycle is done. The buffer fills no faster than their Intake. Beams burn from it while on, and rest when it's empty until it reaches the relight level.
  • Fuel engines need propellant from a touching Fuel Tank; touching tanks share. Tanks refill from the hold.
