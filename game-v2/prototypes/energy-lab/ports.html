Rules this lab follows
  • Power moves only out of an out port (▲) into an in port (▼) across a shared edge. Ports are on specific edges of each part and turn with it (R rotates, T flips). Hull and armour have none.
  • Generators give a fixed amount through each out port every second. A port facing nothing, or facing a part with no in port there, wastes its share.
  • Stores (batteries, capacitors, the core) take through their in ports, up to each port's rate and the space left, and give through their out ports, up to each port's rate and what they hold. What comes in and isn't given stays as charge. Two in ports and one out joins lines; one in and two out splits one.
  • Conduits are tiny stores with one in and one out: one-way lines, straight or cornered.
  • Weapons fill a buffer through their in ports and fire when it holds a shot. Short of supply they fire slower, never weaker. A gun with two ports fed on one gets half. Passive drain leaves the buffer every second whether it fires or not: below it the gun never charges. Beams burn while lit and relight at half. Clips are magazines: empty, they refill the whole clip before firing.
  • Engines burn from a buffer that refills through their ports; empty, they run at intake ÷ burn. Shields draw a flat rate.
  • Each tick every giver offers across its links from what it had at the start of the tick, every taker accepts up to its space, then everything moves at once. Order never matters.
  • Power profiles. Some guns shut their ports for a moment after each shot (discharge) or when a beam burns out (rest): power arriving then is lost unless a store holds it. Some take power only from lines rated at least so much together (threshold): join lines to feed them. Some need an unbroken rate while charging (steady), or the charge collapses.
  • Feed. Tanks hold a consumable; pipes join anything with a feed point (purple squares), on any side. A pipe can’t cross a power line: a Passthrough carries power back to front and feed side to side in one cell. A Combiner takes power at its back and feed on its side and gives one flavoured line: every gun downstream fires with the feed’s effect, at its share of the gun’s power. Feed guns (Scorcher, Slurry Cannon) have their own feed port and fire whatever they’re fed, stronger. A Scrubber takes the flavour off. Each consumable gives something and costs something, and does something nasty when its tank is hit.
  • Break destroys a part. Generators explode; a charged store explodes in proportion to its charge (0.6 × held, radius 1.5 + held ÷ 300); blasts set off their neighbours.
