The rules as built
  1. Power runs from reactors through conduits and modifiers to guns. A modifier counts for a gun only if it sits on a path the power takes to that gun. One on a stub off the side does nothing.
  2. A modifier treats a set amount of energy each second, by its tier. What matters is the share of the gun's draw that gets treated: 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% give levels 1 to 4.
  3. A modifier feeding several guns divides what it treats among them by their draw. One Pip gets the full effect, four Pips get a quarter each.
  4. A bigger gun draws more, so the same level needs a bigger or more modifiers. A tier 1 part fills a Pip and does nothing for a Longbarrel.
  5. Four components give one property each: Coil gives Velocity, Resonator gives Rapid, Chamber gives Explosive, Prism gives Split.
  6. Two components that touch each other fuse into a combination: Coil and Prism give Pierce, Prism and Resonator give Bounce, Chamber and Resonator give Wave. A fused pair treats as much as its smaller half and gives up its two single effects.
  7. Most properties move damage rather than add it. Pierce spreads a shot through the cells behind the first. Explosive trades direct damage for a blast. Split and Rapid do add damage; Rapid also raises the draw, which thins every share on that gun.
  8. Each modifier burns a little power just being on the line, and its price is counted against the guns it feeds. That total is what a death costs.
  9. There is no cap on how many properties a gun can carry. Room on the grid and reactor supply are the limits.
