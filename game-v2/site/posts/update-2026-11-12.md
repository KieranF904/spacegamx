**Building in the world**

- Fit a fabricator or refiner to your ship and build away from the station: refine what you mine and fabricate parts in the field
- Field plans let you queue what you need; half the materials come back when you recycle a part
- Material packs replace repair kits
- Refineries and fabricators run several jobs at once, from engineering skill
- Queue as many orders as you like; they start when materials arrive

**Weapons**

- Weapon modifiers: fit modifier blocks behind a gun to change its shots. Velocity, Rapid, Explosive, Split, Pierce, Wave, Chain, Heavy, Burn and Hyper, up to four on a gun
- Modifiers work together: piercing shots burst going into a hull and coming out, fire spreads from part to part, lightning arcs from every ship a shot passes through
- Gun range at stations: try any weapon you can fit, with any modifiers. A door at the far end leads to a darker room packed with small ships
- Weapon groups: up to five. 1–5 picks the group your left click fires, Shift+1–5 your right click
- New weapon: the Mauler, a heavy cannon firing a slow shell that bursts on impact

**Ships and loot**

- Destroyed ships are now lost and go back to your hangar to rebuild; queue every missing part in one go
- Respawn in a backup ship of your choice; the starter is always free
- Your wreck holds your cargo and some salvage: yours for 5 minutes, then anyone's
- Station repair banks fix damaged parts more cheaply than rebuilding
- Enemies drop from loot tables, including components Sol didn't have before
- Pickup panel (F), mining mode that collects ore for you, and an auto-pickup list with templates
- Item catalogue: search any item and see where it comes from

**Flying and controls**

- Park near a moving station or planet and your ship stays with it. Log out there and you come back beside it
- Quick items moved from 1–5 to C V B N M; the map is now on ; and everything can be rebound

**New pilots**

- A training yard above Vesta teaches flying, mining, looting and building in the field. Skip it any time
- Play as a guest and register any time to keep your progress

**Community**

- You can now support the game with a donation. No ads, nothing for sale, no pay to win
- Donate $10 or more in total for the contributor rank: leave notes and suggestions in the world, comment and vote on others'
